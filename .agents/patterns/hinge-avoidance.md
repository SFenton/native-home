# Hinge avoidance

> **What:** how content stays off the iPhone Duo's hinge band and keeps a readable width on iPad. **Read when:** laying out a two-pane screen, a centred column, a single wide card, a grid or a page sheet.

Decided 2026-10-24 (`duo-ipad-fold-layout`, every section's recommendation); posture rule binding 2026-11-01. **Partly folded** (book pose), landscape: the hinge is a vertical band (455.3 to 495.7 pt, 40.4 pt wide, measured on the simulator) and content keeps clear of it. **Lying flat there is no band and nothing avoids the hinge**: tiles have no middle gap, single objects centre in the free space (the window less the trailing rail), splits divide at that midpoint with a 1 pt band. The band is `EnvironmentValues.windowHinge`, set only while partly folded; `windowFold` carries the hinge in both poses for presence checks (see [split-view](../design/apple/split-view.md), Hinge posture rule). A change of pose animates the reflow (`MotionTokens.standard`; `quick` under Reduce Motion). Everywhere else there is no vertical band (iPad, iPhone, folded Duo, unfolded portrait where the hinge is horizontal), so nothing here changes there. Never branch on a device name; the band comes from `EnvironmentValues.windowHinge` and the view's own frame.

**Two poses (owner, 2026-11-01; UIKit port shipped).** The band is the hinge **only while the inner display is partly folded**. Lying flat there is no band: nothing avoids the hinge, a row of like elements (the Doors tiles) has no gap in the middle, a single object centres in the free space (the window less the trailing rail), and splits and sheets divide at that midpoint with a 1 pt band. UIKit: `UKDeviceSignals` publishes `windowFold` (`WindowFold`, the hinge in either pose: what the shell style and sheets read for presence) and `windowHinge = windowFold.band` (what every avoidance consumer reads: `UKDynamicGrid`, `UKHingedPanes`, `UKOnDemandSplit`, the login form, the Vacuums divider); `UKHinge.frame` mirrors the band for the Rooms-lane views. A posture change reflows with the standard spring through `UKReflow`.

Flat: equal halves at the container midpoint with a 1 pt band. Otherwise (iPad) equal halves with a gutter. A hinge that would leave a pane under 360 pt is ignored. Never halve `proxy.size.width`. |

Flat and everywhere else: the page's own layout, unchanged. The status rail spans the top (exception). Owner, 2026-11-03. |
| Page-level split with navigation | `OnDemandSplitStack` (`SplitPolicy.geometry`) | As before; flat, the divider is the container midpoint (1 pt band). |
| Tiles | `DynamicGridLayout` (`DynamicGridMetrics(... band:)`) | Lying flat the tracks stay even in count (symmetric about the midpoint) with no gap (the Doors row on Security). While the band crosses the grid it is two regions of equal track counts, the gap between the two middle tracks being the band itself. Spans across the band take the band too. A side too narrow for one `minCellWidth` falls back to the even-column rule. |
| A single object that cannot split (alarm card, climate dial, sign-in form) | `.avoidsHinge()` (`HingeAvoidance.placement`) | Stays centred (flat: centred, no avoidance); only when the band crosses its centred frame does it move to the centre of the leading region, narrowed to fit. Off-centre on the Duo only (owner). |
| A page sheet's form (Auto-Reset, Presence overrides) | `.clearsHinge()` (`HingeAvoidance.clearRegion`) | The form uses the wider side of the band; a sheet is placed by the system (`presentationPlacement(.leading)`, below), not offset by us. Shipped on the simulator measurement; re-check on a device. |

- Pure geometry lives in `HassDesign/HingeAvoidance.swift` (`band(in:hinge:)`, `placement`, `clearRegion`) and `SplitPolicy.columnWidths`, with unit tests (`HingeAvoidanceTests`, `DynamicGridBandTests`, `ColumnWidthsTests`).
- Containers are measured in `.global` coordinates, the hinge's space, from a frame that does not depend on the placement (position and width only), so the result cannot feed back into itself.
- `readsWindowHinge()` wraps the whole root (tabs and sign-in), so the login form reads it too.
- **Readable width.** The Security *sheet* and Quick Links detail cap the alarm card at 560 pt (`SecurityPage.alarmWidth`). **The Security page's card takes the full content width (owner, 2026-10-30)** on the iPhone, the folded Duo, the unfolded Duo in portrait and in landscape, and the iPad (designer decision: the mode buttons then sit on the same four columns as the Doors tiles below, and a card shifted off the fold left half the window empty). Lying flat the card spans the page; **partly folded it is a leading section with its four modes in one row** (owner request 2026-11-03, [section-sides](../design/section-sides.md); this replaced the 2 + 2 split around the band). HIG Layout (alignment, readable margins). Climate (640 / 720) and the grids already cap.
- Section grids that sit side by side (Room, Away) use `@ScaledMetric` minimums so they stack at accessibility sizes.
- **Section grids and page-level grids (UIKit, owner 2026-11-01).** The room page's section grid (`UKRoomsGrid(minCellWidth: 340, maxColumns: 2)`), the Media page's, the tile grids inside every section and every other page grid are `UKDynamicGridView`s with `followsFold`: partly folded a two-column section grid is one column on each side of the band (leading column ends at the band, trailing starts after it); **pages with sections no longer use it while partly folded: `UKFoldColumns` places each section wholly on one side, at every text size ([section-sides](../design/section-sides.md), owner 2026-11-03), so no section is full width across the band and its tiles never split around it**; lying flat the columns are even and symmetric about the free-space midpoint, with no gap. The Recipes card grid takes the same tracks (`DynamicGridMetrics`) in its compositional layout. Not followed on purpose: sheet grids (`avoidsHinge = false`; a sheet sits in the leading half) and vertical lists (Inventory rows, todo rows), which do not place content side by side.
- The compact-window menu button follows the size class (`ShellChrome.resolve`), not the idiom; the rail's header fade clears the status region only (`HeaderEdgeFade.edgeCap`).
- **Sheets open in the leading half when the Duo is unfolded in landscape (owner, 2026-10-31: "Modals still opening centered when unfolded").** Every sheet and modal (Quick Links, Your Summary, task and item editors, camera viewer, Security and contacts, room-device and settings sheets) is placed by the system, not by us. iOS 27 has `UISheetPresentationController.preferredPlacement` (`.automatic`, `.leading`, `.center`, `.trailing`; the header says it is ignored when `sourceView` is non-nil) and SwiftUI `View.presentationPlacement(_:)` on the sheet's content ([article](https://sarunw.com/posts/presentation-placement-on-iphone-duo/), iOS 27.1 SDK). Unfolded, `.automatic` centres the sheet and `.leading` puts it against the left edge; partly folded the system keeps the sheet off the fold whatever is asked (`.automatic`, `.leading`, `.center` all go to the left half); on the folded outer screen and on a phone without a hinge placement changes nothing. **Rule:** on a device with a hinge (`DuoSheetPolicy.deviceHasHinge` / `HingePresence`, or a hinge region visible) every sheet asks for `.leading`; iPad and iPhone keep `.automatic`, so an iPad sheet stays centred. `.trailing` is not used (it moves the controls into the vertical bar). Never set a `sourceView` on a sheet (a zoom `preferredTransition` source is not one). **UIKit port:** one helper, `UKSheetPlacement.place(_:in:)` (`UIKitPort/Design/UKSheetPlacement.swift`), called by `UKHassModalController.configureSheet`, `UKShellSheet` and the header-sheet path; nothing sets a sheet's width or frame (the earlier `.custom` presentation, `UKSheetPresentationController` and width override are gone). **SwiftUI:** `hassSheet` (`HassSheetPresentation`) applies `presentationPlacement(.leading)` through `SheetPlacementModifier`, and every sheet goes through `hassSheet`. A new presentation site must go through those. Unfolded portrait: see [modal-sheet](modal-sheet.md).
- **Sheets fill the height on the folded and landscape Duo (owner, 2026-10-31).** Where the window has a hinge and either no division region is visible (folded) or the band is vertical (landscape), every sheet opens full height from the bottom, with the grabber and no zoom; unfolded portrait is unchanged. The rule and its sites: [modal-sheet](modal-sheet.md), `DuoSheetPolicy`. In unfolded landscape the sheet is still placed in the leading half (above).
- **Todo pages** (Chores, Groceries, each resident, Unassigned, Home Improvement, admin To-Do): where `SplitPolicy.allowsSplit`, the list is the leading pane and the Add form or a row's edit form opens in the trailing pane (`todo.pane`, placeholder `todo.pane.placeholder`); elsewhere those forms are sheets. The forms close the pane through `EnvironmentValues.inlineSheetClose`. Choosing another row replaces the open form.

- **Section sides (partly folded, owner 2026-11-03).** While `UKShellEnvironment.windowHinge` is non-nil and `SplitPolicy.geometry` gives each side at least 360 pt, every section of Home, Security, Climate, Custom Lights, Sprinklers, the room pages and Media sits wholly on one side of the band. `UKFoldColumns` (`UIKitPort/Design`) lays the sections out by hand from the pure `FoldColumns` (`App/Layout`: geometry, side parity, frames, reading order); a pose change only moves frames inside `UKReflow` (pages whose flat form is a grid move their sections out of it in `onSplitChange`, starting from the old frames). Sides, the Cameras rule and the wrapped-rail rule are in `.agents/design/section-sides.md`. A new page with sections uses `UKFoldColumns`; it never adds a second columns container. **Tests (2026-11-04).** `UKHostedSectionSidesTests` asserts the partly folded layout at the measured 868 pt content (every page, Large, xLarge and AX5; the scrolling rail may span at default sizes, a wrapped one never); the XCUITest `SectionSidesJourneyTests` cannot, because XCUITest drives the Duo's outer panel (466 x 678 pt) even while the inner one is lit, so it runs one never-skipping journey that checks the band when it sees the split and the single column otherwise (an `XCTSkip` on the Duo hangs the runner and the restart returns it folded). **First-layout exception.** The initial pose is set on the first layout pass of a never-laid-out `UKFoldColumns`: nothing is on screen yet, so nothing animates and no value flips; every later change is decided outside layout (`UKFoldColumns.updateSplit`, `UKFoldColumns.splitWritesInLayout` counts violations).

- **Owned files** (also in `contracts/patterns.json`): `WindowHingeEnvironment.swift`; `UKFoldColumns.swift`, `FoldColumns.swift`.

- **Leading-region mode (2026-11-06).** `UKHingeClearingView(content:leadingOnly:active:)` can take the leading region of the band instead of its wider side (`leadingOnly`), and with `leadingOnly` it lets touches through where the content takes none; `active: false` makes it a plain full-size host. `UKStateMessageOverlay(leadingOnHinge:)` uses it and defaults to off; only the house-layout gate turns it on, so other state messages keep their centring until a separate change adopts it.
