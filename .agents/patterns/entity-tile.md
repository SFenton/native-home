# Entity tile

> **What:** the glass tile used for entity controls. **Read when:** showing an entity as a tappable tile.

Built on `claude/action-nav-tiles` (in review): actions, chevrons with actions and read-only faces follow [action-tiles](action-tiles.md). The fan's Lock and Power and `BrightnessTile`'s power button are now `TileActionButtonStyle` discs placed by `TileActionStrip`; `FanRailButton` and the accessory rail described below no longer exist for them (the generic `accessory` slot stays for custom controls such as `TileVolumeSlider`). Every `.state` tile is flat (no sheen, rim or press response). Read the accessory-rail and bathroom-fan bullets below as history for geometry only.

- `GlassTile(title:subtitle:symbol:family:isActive:)` draws one of direction G's faces (`TileFaceStyle`, [direction-g](../design/direction-g.md) Colour families): **off** is dark (the page module, or `#2C2C2E` in a sheet via `tileSurface`, which `modalChrome()` sets) with the glyph in `ColorFamily.offGlyph`; **lit** is a light that is on, the warm tile with dark ink and a bright band as wide as the brightness; **filled** is any other device that is on, in its family fill with the family ink (progress is a lighter band from the leading edge: with white ink the part still to go is shaded 18% black, never a white band, which measured 4.2:1 under the state on cleaning purple; power's dark ink gets a 25% white band); **error** (`isError:`) is a red glyph and red state on the dense ground, and VoiceOver reads the state. Callers pick a `family`; `tint` is optional and only colours the glyph and rim of the **tinted** face that callers without a family still get (kept so feature files compile unchanged until their G lane picks a family).
- Semantics follow the web UX contract: chevrons only for navigate/modal; every tile is one element with the `.isButton` role (toggles add `.isToggle` and their on/off value). `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` drops the inner `Button`'s role, so `GlassTile` restates it; without it iOS 27 exposes the tile as `Other` and `app.buttons[id]` misses it (found 2026-10-11 on Security); unavailable entities are dimmed and not tappable.
- **Light sheets** ([direction-g](../design/direction-g.md) Layers: Sheets): `GlassTileFace` resolves a `TileAppearance` from `tileSurface` and the environment colour scheme; only a `.sheet` tile under the light scheme is light, and pages are always dark. The off, tinted and error faces then use the white cell, `Ink.primary` titles, `Surface.secondaryInSheet` state ink, the family's `offGlyphRGB(.light)` glyph (a darker variant of the family colour, 3:1 or better on white and the ground), `#C21717` for errors, a 22% wash, and a tint darkened by `TileFaceStyle.lightGlyph(forTint:)`. The lit tile and the filled family faces keep their fixed fills and inks in both appearances. Use `ColorFamily.glyph` and `.offGlyph` (they adapt) for glyphs on a cell or row; use `Color(rgb: family.spec.glyph)` only where the colour is a fixed fill (`PrimaryButtonStyle`).
- **Layout** (`HassDesign/TileLayout`, owner 2026-10-16: "it feels too high up / lots of dead space, but I like the tiles themselves"): `GlassTileFace` draws one of four arrangements chosen by `EnvironmentValues.tileLayout`. The shipped **row** puts the glyph in a fixed 30 pt column and the title and state beside it, the pair vertically centred, with a 48 pt content height (72 pt tile, Apple Home's accessory tile). At accessibility text sizes a row becomes **anchored bottom** (glyph top-leading, text bottom-leading, 72 pt content): a row has no width left for text at AX5. `centeredStack` stays for the gallery's comparison (Tile Layouts on `FENTON_GALLERY=direction-g`). `stackedTop` also draws the vacuum sheet's read-only Status, Battery and Dock tiles (caption over value, `.inert`; owner, 2026-10-28), which sit in one column at accessibility sizes. `BrightnessTile` and every other entity tile draw through the same face, so there is nothing else to keep in step.
- **Chevron.** A `.navigate` tile (or a `BrightnessTile(onOpen:)`) draws its chevron vertically centred on the trailing edge in every layout, and the text keeps `chevronReserve` clear of it. It is no longer in the glyph row.
- **Room tiles** (`fillRGB:`, `TileFaceStyle.colored`): a caller-owned solid fill from `RoomPalette` (the Rooms grid and Climate's Advanced Controls rooms). White ink and a filled white glyph on or off, the same in both appearances; tinted glass gives it the translucent fill and highlight on a page and, in a sheet, only the bottom shade and the hairline. Error and unavailable still win. Filled family tiles (on) already draw the fill symbol variant in white, so a device that is on reads as a filled white glyph; off tiles keep the outline family glyph.
- **Navigation tiles reserve two title lines** (`.tileLayout(.anchoredBottom).tileTitleLines(2)`, `TileMetrics.minContentHeight`; owner, 2026-10-16: "Rooms should have taller tiles with room for two lines of text"). The Rooms grid, Climate's Rooms tab and the Quick Links grid use it with `DynamicGridLayout(equalRowHeights: true)` and `.equalHeightTiles()`: the glyph top-leading, the name on the bottom line (a one-line name sits on the bottom line, a two-line name fills both), one height for every tile (glyph + 8 pt + two lines of subheadline + padding, 96 pt at the default size, from `ScaledMetric`s so it grows with Dynamic Type). `.anchoredBottom` has no stack spacing, so the spacer's 8 pt minimum is the whole gap and the reserved height is the drawn height. Device tiles on room pages keep the Row.
- **Quick Links tiles** (`QuickLinkPalette`, `GlassTile(fillRGB:)`): solid fill, filled white glyph, white ink, one colour per link from its own page's family (Rooms is the one new navy). Quick Links was unequal because it had no `equalRowHeights`: with the Row layout (#95) a tile with a state line was taller than one without.
- **Finish: tinted glass** (owner, 2026-10-17: option A of the Tile Finish proposals; `HassDesign/TileFinish`, `TintedGlassGround`, `TintedGlassRim`, `TintedGlassSheen`; tokens and measurements in [direction-g](../design/direction-g.md), Tile finish). Every tile draws it by default: the family or room fill at 78% over a dark underlay on a page (opaque in a sheet), one sheen gradient (white 14% at the top to clear at 46% of the height to black 8% at the bottom), a 1 pt hairline lit at the top (white 32% to 6%), and no live blur or material, so no backdrop layer per tile. The off and error grounds keep the thin and dense modules (the underlay alone) with the same sheen and rim; the lit tile and power stay opaque with dark ink (a translucent light fill over a dark wallpaper only gets darker); a sheet has no wallpaper, so fills are opaque there and a light sheet's white cell takes the separator colour as its hairline and no sheen. A room or Quick Link fill in a sheet gets only the bottom shade (its white ink sits within 0.2 of 4.5:1 flat, so a highlight would break it). The off glyph keeps 88% of its family colour on a page and is lifted toward white until it reads 3:1 on the lightest ground (`ColorFamily.offGlyphRGB(_:under:)`; the muted Food families need it), state text on a page takes `secondaryOnWallpaper` (85%). **Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast resolve to the solid finish** (`TileFinish.effective`), the documented fallback, which is also the gallery's second row. Controls that are not a `GlassTile` but show a chosen fill (the alarm mode buttons) take the same edge with `.tintedGlassEdge(cornerRadius:)`; do not copy the sheen or rim into a screen. `tileFinish(_:)` and `FENTON_TILE_FINISH` (Debug, `solid` or `tintedGlass`) exist for the gallery and the scroll cost probe.
- **First configure (owner bug, 2026-11-05, [sheet-reveal](../design/sheet-reveal.md) rule 7).** A tile's glyph is drawn, never animated in, on its first appearance. `UKGlassTileFaceView` runs a symbol content transition (`.replace`) only when the face is in a window and has already drawn (`window != nil`, the same guard as the colour cross-fade). A consumer passes its **real** first model (symbol included) to `UKGlassTileView.init` and observes later changes; it never builds with a placeholder symbol (`"circle"`) and configures the real one afterwards. That placeholder made every Home chip sheet's glyphs pop in about 1 s late, one by one. A live change of symbol on screen still uses `.replace` (none under Reduce Motion).
- **Haptics and failure (owner, 2026-10-24).** A tap on a `.toggle`, `.command` or `.selection` tile plays `TapFeedback.tap` (`GlassTileKind.playsTapFeedback`; a `.navigate` tile does not, the transition is its feedback), through `tapFeedback(trigger:)` ([tap-feedback](tap-feedback.md)). When `isError` turns true the tile plays the `.error` haptic and announces "Title, subtitle". A command that failed passes `isError: true` and the subtitle "Didn't Respond" for 4 s ([optimistic-actions](optimistic-actions.md), Failure); the same face serves a jammed lock and a garage door in error.
- **No redundant state line on contact sensors (owner, 2026-10-30).** A contact sensor tile (the Contact Sensors sheet's room cards and per-sensor tiles, Climate's Open Contact Sensors) draws no "Open" / "Closed" / "Door Closed" text: the glyph and fill say it. `RoomOverview.tileSubtitle` and `RoomFormat.contactTileSubtitle` return nothing for Open and Closed but still show Unavailable and Unknown; VoiceOver still reads the full state (`spokenValue` / `tileAccessibilityValue`). Any new contact tile uses those helpers.
- **App tiles (Media / remote quick launch, Streaming A, Streaming B...; owner, 2026-10-30)** draw no "Playing" line: the media ring says it and VoiceOver reads "Playing". They sit in the standard tile grid, so the artwork grows with its track up to 200 pt (`AppTile.maximumArtwork`, was 168).
- Tile size stays stable while values update.
- Disabled (`.disabled`) tiles keep full-contrast text: `GlassTileButtonStyle` never fades the label, and the face mutes only the icon (secondary) and border. A locked tile still carries state ("Away", "Select the user … first"); `.plain` faded it to 2.5:1 and failed the contrast audit (Solo Trip, iPhone, 2026-10-11). VoiceOver still reads it as dimmed.
- Unavailable (`isUnavailable`) tiles follow the same rule: the face halves the opacity of the glyph and the tinted stroke only. The title, the `Unavailable` value and the ground stay at full strength, because dimming the ground lets the wallpaper show through behind white text. A whole-face fade left the text at 2.4:1, and the signed-in Security tab failed its audit on the folded Duo before states arrived (2026-10-14). `AlarmModeButton` matches. The Security gallery's `FENTON_GALLERY_SCENARIO=unavailable` audits it.
- Every entity tile draws through `GlassTileFace`; `BrightnessTile` (`HassDesign/BrightnessTile.swift`, web `LightBrightnessCard.tsx`) is the dimmable-light variant. Tap flips power, a horizontal drag sets brightness from the finger's position and commits on release, a vertical drag scrolls. The fill is the light's `rgb_color` (else the light tint) as a 32% wash so labels keep contrast. VoiceOver: activate flips power, adjust steps 10% (the web keyboard steps 5%; 10% keeps VoiceOver swipes practical). Value reads `Off`, `45%` or `Unavailable`. On iOS 18+ the drag is a UIKit pan (`HorizontalPan`) that only begins on a mostly horizontal movement. A SwiftUI `DragGesture`, even attached as a simultaneous gesture, stops a parent `ScrollView` from scrolling when the drag starts on a tile (found 2026-10-11: Custom Lights could not scroll to Reset All Lights). Gesture and optimistic state live in the pure `BrightnessInteraction` value type so tap, drag and adjust are unit-tested; the view only wires gestures and the pending timeout to it.
- **Power button.** A `BrightnessTile(onOpen:)` (a tap opens details) draws a 44 pt power button at the trailing edge in place of the chevron (`BrightnessTile.powerButton`, label "Turn Off/On {title}", identifier `brightness.power`), so the primary action is visible and reachable without a drag (HIG Gestures; hig-decisions VoiceOver bucket). The tile's own VoiceOver custom action is dropped then; the button is the element.
- The dragger (direction G): `BrightnessTile` draws the lit face. While a horizontal drag is in progress the tile lifts (scale 1.03 and a shadow; shadow only with Reduce Motion), a `BrightnessBubble` ("30%") floats over the band's edge, kept inside the tile and hidden from VoiceOver, and `.sensoryFeedback(.selection)` ticks each time the drag crosses a 10% step (`hapticStep`).
- `BrightnessTile(onOpen:)` makes a tap open the light's details instead of flipping power (web `tapAction: 'more-info'`, Custom Lights): the tile shows a chevron, VoiceOver's activate opens details and a "Turn On"/"Turn Off" custom action flips power; drag and adjust still set brightness.
- Light power is always an explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off` from the shown state via `LightControl` (`HassCore/Lights`), never `toggle`: Presence Based Lighting's Presence Lock reverts toggles in empty rooms and only honors a bare `turn_on` from an app user. Brightness 0 sends `turn_off`.
- Kinds (`GlassTileKind`): `.toggle` (`.isToggle`, On/Off), `.navigate` (chevron), `.command`, `.selection(isSelected:)` (`.isSelected` while chosen, web `aria-pressed`), `.state` (read-only: not a button, never dimmed as disabled). An action tile (`behavior: .perform`) whose primary item is a choice (`TileActionItem.indicator: .choice`, [action-tiles](action-tiles.md)) is a `.selection` tile.
- A tile with an accessory takes its identifier through `.tileIdentifier(_:)`, never `.accessibilityIdentifier` on the whole tile: that one also lands on the accessory's buttons and hides their identifiers (bathroom fan Lock and Power, 2026-10-12).
- Optional `wash` (a state color over the material, e.g. towel rack on, bed heating), `progress` / `progressTint` (leading fill, e.g. dishwasher), and a trailing `accessory` view builder for in-tile controls (bathroom fan lock and power) that sit outside the tile's button; the face keeps room for it.
- **Accessory rail** (owner, 2026-10-17: "the chevron is drawn on top of the Power button"). `GlassTile` places the rail, never the caller, and only the fan uses one today. At regular text sizes the rail is **vertically centred** on the trailing edge, one chevron slot in from it: its trailing padding is the tile padding plus `GlassTileFace.chevronReserve` (20 pt) on a `.navigate` tile, so reading right to left is the chevron, a gap, then the buttons, and the two never overlap. The face reserves the rail's measured width plus 8 pt, so text stops short of it (nothing when the rail is empty, as when a fan is unavailable). At accessibility text sizes the rail drops to the **bottom-leading corner under the text** (the face reserves its height) instead of squeezing the text into a sliver. The chevron stays centred on the trailing edge in every case. Any new tile with an accessory inherits this; do not hand-offset one. Add `.tileIdentifier(_:)` for the tile's own id.
- `.tileAccessibilityLabel(_:)` replaces the title as the VoiceOver label when the title alone is not what to speak (a vacuum room tile: "Kitchen, cleaning order 1").
- `.tileAccessibilityValue(_:)` replaces the subtitle as the VoiceOver value when the text is shortened for the eye (the fan: text "Off in 12:30", spoken "on, off in 12 minutes, locked"). `.tileSingleLineSubtitle()` (environment `tileSubtitleSingleLine`) keeps the subtitle to one line that shrinks to 85% before it truncates; use it for a live status line.
- **Bathroom fan tile** (`HassUI/Features/Rooms/BathroomFanRoomTile.swift`, owner design 2026-10-17): the air family; status is one line, `On`, `Off`, `Off in 12:30` (a `TimelineView` ticks each second only while `showsCountdown`; it ticks hourly otherwise so the tile keeps its identity) or `Unavailable`; lock is never in the text. Two action discs (`TileActionButtonStyle`, formerly `FanRailButton`), Lock then Power, ids `room.card.Fan.action.lock|power`, 44 pt circles that scale with Dynamic Type up to 1.5x with the glyph held under 45% of the circle: inactive is translucent (white 12% on the dark off tile, black 18% on the lit teal) with a white glyph, active (locked, or the fan on) is a solid white circle with the glyph in the air fill `#006E62`; locked draws `lock.fill`, unlocked `lock.open`. An unavailable fan hides both buttons. At regular width a lead-row fan joins the two-column grid (`RoomSectionView.leadJoinsGrid`); a phone keeps the full-width lead row.
- A tappable tile is a `GlassTile` with its own `action` (push through a `NavigationStack` path when it opens a page). Never put a tile drawn with `.allowsHitTesting(false)` inside a `Button` or `NavigationLink` label: a label with no hit-testable content leaves the outer control untappable (Quick Links and the room grid ignored every tap on iPhone, 2026-10-11). `.allowsHitTesting(false)` is only for read-only faces (`MediaRemoteTabs` state tiles).
- `GlassStateTile` (feature-local in `HassUI/Features/Settings/Household`; the shared read-only kind is now `GlassTile(kind: .state)`, so new read-only tiles use that) draws the same face with no action, for values with no action (web `EntityActionCard` without an action, e.g. car). VoiceOver reads the title and the shown state.
- Put a tappable tile's accessibility label, value and traits on its `Button` directly, never behind `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)`: that wrapper leaves the Button exposed beside a second element, so an identifier set by the caller, the value and the disabled state land on the non-button one and `app.buttons[id]` finds nothing (found 2026-10-11 on the household Settings tiles). `SelectableOptionRow` follows the same rule.
- Thermostat and bed-side tiles take their face from `ThermostatTileFace` (`HassDesign`), the one rule for both: heating and cooling are filled; idle or off is the off face with the Heating glyph (heat mode, or a bed level of zero or more) or the Cooling glyph (any other mode, or a negative bed level), never neutral grey (2026-10-15). `RoomTileFace.resolve` (room cards) and `BedroomStyle.face` (`BedSideTile`) both call it; a new thermostat or bed tile must too. Read-only temperature sensor tiles (the Climate chip sheet) are not thermostats and keep the `.climate` family.

- **Owned files** (also in `contracts/patterns.json`): `ColorFamily+Glass.swift`, `FinishColor.swift`, `GlassTileParts.swift`, `LitTile.swift`, `TileFaceStyle+Finish.swift`, `TileSurface.swift`, `TintedGlassEdge.swift`, `TileFaceInk.swift`, `TileTextFit.swift`.

- `entity-tile/no-light-toggle`: Light power is an explicit turn_on / turn_off from the shown state, never toggle (entity-tile). Scans `**/*.swift`.
