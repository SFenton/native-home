# Room device sheets

> **What:** the device sheets opened from room tiles: bathroom fan, air quality (purifier), humidifier, grill. **Read when:** adding or changing one of them, or a new device sheet that follows the same shape.

Web source: `ha-sfenton-react-dash` `src/components/hass/BathroomFanModalContent.tsx`, `AirPurifierModalContent.tsx`, `HumidifierModalContent.tsx`, `GrillModalContent.tsx` (spec: `plans/room-devices-spec.md` in the project files).

- **Layers.** Config (`HassCore/Config/RoomDeviceConfig.swift`) names the entities per room. One controller per device in `HassCore/RoomDevices/` reads `EntityStore` and sends through `HassServiceCalling` via `RoomDeviceCommands` (optimistic overlay, reset on failure). Views in `HassUI/Features/RoomDevices/` take a controller, so pages and the gallery share them.
- **Configured atoms (schema 2).** `ServerUIRoomDeviceDescriptor` requires explicit targets for `air-purifier@1`, `bathroom-fan@1`, `grill@1` and `humidifier@1`. `room-device-control` selects one typed atom, authored options/order/copy/icon/native color family, never a sheet or arbitrary service. `ServerUIRoomDeviceSource` injects the store/caller/schedule storage and revalidates domain intents.
  `RoomDeviceCommands.hasPendingIntent` includes the actual call lifetime, even after optimism expires; a publication cannot remove/rebind a pending source or control. A locally changed timer selection or focused control blocks incompatible publication rather than being reset.
- **Fan room atom.** `fan-room` uses the canonical two-rail provider, with required authored `fanRail` order/symbols/verbs and a `modalId` resolved through the existing Shell presentation action. The order must contain Lock and Power exactly once. `labels.action` is its countdown prefix; `labels.on/off` are its state labels. Body navigation never sends a power intent. Countdown redraws stop off-window.
  Device sources expose only the typed string projections `stateText` (canonical domain formatting) and `phase`; read-only status does not expose a raw service path. Individual humidifier info values require their own sensors, not just an available power switch.
- **Extraction, not a second feature.** `UKFanTimerPicker` and `UKFanTimerRunning` have value/closure initializers; the existing controller initializers preserve all canonical callers.
  Fields disable during save. `UKDeviceStatusPill`, `UKSegmentedChoice`, `UKControllerToggle`, `UKControllerStepper`, `UKGlassTileView` and `UKThermostatDial` remain the native primitives (HIG: [Controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/controls), [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), [Feedback](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/feedback)). No new layout/scroll/animation machinery or visual redesign.
- **Schedule integration boundary.** `ServerUIHumidifierActivityDraft` owns only domain validation, conflicts and save semantics. Shell must retain it by source/rule, preserve focus, guard dirty/busy navigation and supply collection-item routing and anchored confirmation. `DeleteTicket` binds the exact descriptor/rule and is revalidated after confirmation.
  The new adapter requires a verified administrator for editing; legacy feature callers retain their unknown-role behavior. `humidifier-schedule-field` and `humidifier-schedule-activities` are explicitly non-publishable capability gaps until those Shell interfaces land. The scheduling switch alone negotiates `sh.humidifier-scheduling@1`.
- **Isolated schedule reads.** `UKServerUIRenderer.scheduleStorage` forwards the narrow `HassScheduleStoring` injection. An explicit caller always wins over a connection; a rejecting preview caller never causes schedule storage to fall back to an unrestricted connection. `ServerUIRoomDeviceSource.loadSchedules()` is explicit; activities come from `schedules.rules`, and edits use `ServerUIHumidifierActivityDraft(source:rule:isNew:)`.
- **Authored light and sprinkler assemblies.** `custom-lights@1` and `sprinkler-controller@1` construct retained canonical providers from complete targets, palettes/reset values and controller/zone policy. `UKServerUIDeviceAtom` reuses the existing color picker, brightness controls, sprinkler cards and schedule rows, with width-driven fitting. Color debounce pins the original light through acknowledgement; group confirmation revalidates the captured color and members.
  Sprinkler drafts retain time-row IDs, validate exact HH:mm values and schedule revisions, refuse dirty reload and preserve rejected saves. Pinned Apply and dirty-exit UI belong to the shared shell, not a second form language. HIG: [Color wells](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color-wells), [Pickers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/pickers), [Alerts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts).
- **Chrome.** `RoomDeviceSheetChrome`: NavigationStack, the system inline title with the device status through `.sheetSubtitle(_:)` (`HassDesign/SheetSubtitle.swift`: the system navigation subtitle on iOS 26+, none earlier), the circle-X Close (`device.sheet.done`). Sections are `DeviceSection` (a `PageSectionHeader(_, inSheet: true)`) over an equal-height `DeviceTileGrid` of `GlassTile`s in their device's colour family (fan air, grill cooking).
  Two to four exclusive options are a `SegmentedChoice` (system segmented control, a menu at accessibility sizes; options are `segmentedControls[id].buttons[title]` in UI tests). Cards and pills sit on `HassTokens.Surface.sheetCell`; the one main action per screen is `PrimaryButtonStyle`, secondary buttons are grey bordered capsules, destructive ones plain `Ink.danger` text (direction G).
  Present with `.hassSheet(.form | .page)` (large detent; page sizing for the humidifier).
- **Optimism.** Fan and purifier clear on the next live change with a 2 s revert (web `useOptimisticState`). The humidifier clears only when Home Assistant confirms the value, with an 8 s revert (web humidifier controller).
- **Fan** (owner design, 2026-10-17). Power is the air (teal) family and Lock the security blue (`ColorFamily.securityHome`, no new colour), so the tiles read apart at a glance.
  The timer is a custom segmented row of six lengths (`FanTimerLengthRow`: 5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m, 1h; 44 pt tall; the chosen one is the label's opposite, white with black text on a dark sheet and near-black with white text on a light one, `Surface.selectedSegment`; three columns at accessibility sizes) over one `PrimaryButtonStyle` button, "Turn Off in N Minutes" (or "Turn Off in 1 Hour"), and the footnote "The fan runs, then turns itself off.
  Starting a timer turns it on if it's off." That copy is true: the HA script's `timer_start` ends with `switch.turn_on` (`/config/scripts.yaml`, checked 2026-10-17), so the button is enabled whenever the fan and timer helper are available and the controller shows the fan on at once.
  A running timer (`FanTimerRunning`) is a 40 pt timer glyph and a 40 pt semibold monospaced countdown in the web's timer orange (`Ink.timer`:
  `#FF9F0A` on a dark sheet, `#B35C00` on a light one), no subtitle, a 6 pt orange progress bar that empties as time runs out (`totalSeconds` is the `timer` entity's `duration` attribute, else the shortest offered length that covers what is left), and **Clear Timer** as a red tinted capsule (`TintedCapsuleButtonStyle`).
  **No "Auto-disable lock"**: the app always sends `auto_unlock: true` and never `timer_auto_unlock`, so the lock is released when the timer ends. The script's own `timer_cancel` leaves the lock as it is (it only clears the auto-unlock flag); that is Home Assistant's behaviour and stays untouched. Everything goes through `script.<room>_bathroom_fan_command`.
  The sheet is `.hassSheet(.form)`, fitted to its content.
- **Air quality.** Fan Mode, Auto Mode and Fan Speed are segmented choices; Auto shows the auto sub-modes, Manual shows four speeds (`fan.set_percentage` 0/33/66/100). Subtitle is the AQI / PM2.5 summary.
- **Humidifier.** Mist dial (single-value `ThermostatDial` via `DialValueStyle`, `thermostat-dial.md`) with Turn On/Off; Controls / Schedules / Info is the sheet's bottom `modalTabBar` (`humidifier.tabs`). Two columns (hero beside tabs) only where `SplitPolicy` allows and the window is at least 640 pt wide.
  Schedules edit the `schedule.*` helper over the websocket (`schedule/list`, `schedule/update`); only admins edit, overlaps block Save, the first saved activity turns scheduling on and deleting the last turns it off.
- **Grill.** State header and four cards: probe sensors read-only with units, Keep Warm and Smoke switches.
- **Accessibility.** Tiles are buttons with on/off or selected values; sensor cards are static text. Dial handles are adjustable ("Mist level", "Target humidity", value "50 percent"). The fan countdown speaks as a duration. Every control is reachable at AX5 by scrolling.
- **Departures from web.** Purifier selections are optimistic (the web waits). Grill switches and humidifier switches send explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off`, never `toggle`. Grill sensors show units. The fan's timer button reads "Turn Off in N Minutes" and drops the web's "Auto-disable lock" checkbox.
- **Not here.** Dishwasher (in the spec, separate lane) Room pages wire these sheets in; the bathroom fan's room tile and its Lock and Power rail are in [entity-tile](entity-tile.md).
- **Tests.** Controllers in `HassCoreTests/BathroomFanControllerTests.swift`, `RoomDeviceControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierScheduleTests.swift`; dial style in `HassDesignTests/DialValueStyleTests.swift`; hosted renders and gallery echo in `HassUITests/RoomDeviceViewTests.swift`; the grill sheet's journeys, audits and screenshots in `Apps/iOSUITests/RoomDevicesGrillJourneyTests.swift` and `RoomDevicesCaptureJourneyTests.swift` (over `RoomDevicesJourneyTestCase`) against `FENTON_GALLERY=room-devices` (`FENTON_GALLERY_DEVICE`).
- Header: `RoomDeviceSheetChrome` sets the system inline title and, on iOS 26+, the device status as `navigationSubtitle`, so both follow the bar's Dynamic Type rules. The older custom principal title (headline + caption, `minimumScaleFactor`) shrank at AX5 and failed the humidifier audits (2026-10-13), so it is gone: before iOS 26 the sheet shows the title alone (2026-10-15, shared `.sheetSubtitle`).
