# Dynamic grid

> **What:** the responsive tile grid. **Read when:** laying out tiles, cameras or quick links.

- One layout: `DynamicGridLayout(minCellWidth:maxColumns:spacing:)`. Column count is `floor((width + spacing) / (minCellWidth + spacing))`, clamped to `1...maxColumns`; leftover width is shared equally by all tracks.
- An unbounded proposal (an `HStack` probing a `maxWidth: .infinity` child, as the Food hub's landscape columns do) is treated like no proposal; `DynamicGridMetrics` with an infinite width returns `maxColumns` instead of trapping on `Int(.infinity)`.
- Items may claim more tracks with `.gridSpan(_:)`. An item never spans more columns than exist.
- **Short rows fill (owner, 2026-11-04, [duo-widths](../design/duo-widths.md) rule 1).** A row that leaves tracks unused (a lone tile, a short last row) is filled across its width, or one side's while a hinge band crosses the grid (a band is a wall), and no item gets less than its placed span. **A grid of one row** shares that width equally (`DynamicGridMetrics.sharedWidths`, max-min fair share: three tiles in four tracks become thirds). **A grid of more than one row** fills in whole tracks (`DynamicGridMetrics.fillingPlacements`) so every edge stays on the other rows' track lines: the spare tracks go one at a time to the narrowest item, ties to the later (4 + 3 is 4 + 1, 1, 2; a 1 and a 2 in four are 2, 2). Counts and row membership never change, heights are measured at the filled widths, and an item spanning its row or side is `fullRow`. Done once in `UKGridSolver` (`UKGridConfiguration.fill = .share`, every family), so `UKDynamicGridView`, `UKFlexGridView` and the collection layout agree. `fill: .none` keeps short rows leading: grids under `controlMaxWidth` (automatic), aspect-ratio media (Cameras, Quick App Launch, remote apps), choice chips (`UKChoiceGrid`, Presence options), dials, the scroll-cost workload.
- **Section minimum grows only (duo-widths rule 5).** A section grid (340 pt, `DynamicGridMetrics.sectionMinWidth`: room pages, Media, Vacation) scales its minimum with `.body` as `max(340, scaled)`: text size removes section columns, never adds them, so the 637 pt unfolded Duo portrait is one column at every size and flat landscape (836 pt) two. A lone last section fills both columns by the same fill.
- **Equal row heights** (`equalRowHeights: true`): every tile row takes the tallest tile's height in the whole grid, so one wrapping title (Primary Bedroom Closet on an iPhone) lifts every tile; tiles fill it inside `.equalHeightTiles()` (sets `tileFillsHeight`, read by `GlassTileFace`). A heading placed with `.gridHeader()` takes a whole row and keeps its own height, so one grid can hold several titled runs that share a height. Recomputed on every layout pass (Dynamic Type, rotation). Used by the room overview sheets.
- **Four is a floor on iPad, never a cap** (plan Phase 4, decision 1, 2026-10-15). A full-width tile grid must reach at least 4 columns at iPad Pro 11 portrait (content about 802 pt), and keep growing with width. The standard 150 pt tile (`minCellWidth` 150, 12 pt spacing) fits 5 at 802 pt, so a grid with `maxColumns` 4 or more and that tile width qualifies; a `maxColumns` of 3 does not and is not used on a tile grid. Tile grids pass `maxColumns` 6 unless a design reason bounds them. Recorded exceptions: Chores quick links stop at 4 (five links, one of them two tracks wide, fill four tracks exactly); grids of wide cards (`minCellWidth` 240 and up: Admin tiles, sheet detail cards) keep 2 or 3.
- **Sections side by side count as columns.** Room pages and Media lay two (Media: up to three) 340 pt section columns across (`maxColumns` 2 and 3) with each section's tiles in its own grid, so iPad portrait shows 2 + 2 tiles across: four tiles in a row, with a clear title over each group. That already meets the floor; the pages stay sectioned (web `RoomGrid`, `DashboardViewPage.tsx:284`) rather than flattening to one four-column grid. In landscape the section grid takes 3 columns of 2 tiles.
- **Hinge-aware even columns.** `DynamicGridLayout` read through `callAsFunction` (so every `DynamicGridLayout(...) { }` call) reads `EnvironmentValues.windowHinge` (`HassDesign/WindowHingeEnvironment.swift`, published by the shell's `readsWindowHinge()`). While a hinge exists in either pose (`windowFold.hinge`, or the `windowHinge` band: the unfolded iPhone Duo) the column count rounds down to an even number, never below two: `DynamicGridMetrics(width:minCellWidth:maxColumns:spacing:evenColumns:)` and `DynamicGridMetrics.evenRounded(_:)` (5 becomes 4, 3 becomes 2; a single track stays single). Leftover width is shared by the even tracks, so tiles grow rather than leave a gap. The grid does not know where the fold falls; with the window's content offset by the system's trailing bar the midline may still cross a tile on a one-grid page, which is why Home splits into two real columns on the hinge (`HomeColumns`, [duo](../design/apple/duo.md)). Unit tests: `DynamicGridMetricsTests`, `DynamicGridHingeTests`.
- **Minimums follow text size** (owner, 2026-10-24, option A). A tile grid's `minCellWidth` is a `@ScaledMetric(relativeTo:)` of the tile's own text style (`.subheadline` for tiles, `.footnote` for the camera name capsule), declared by the view that owns the grid; `DynamicGridLayout` stays a dumb container and never reads the text size. At default size nothing changes; at accessibility sizes the grid steps down to fewer, wider tiles, one column on an iPhone, so enlarged words never clip. A literal minimum on a tile grid is a bug. Cameras going from 4 to 2 columns at AX5 on iPad is intended: legibility first. Chores keeps its four-column cap on iPad (the recorded exception above), which the scaled minimum still honours.
- **Band-aware tracks (2026-10-24).** The grid wrapper also measures its own frame and, where the hinge band crosses it, passes the band to `DynamicGridMetrics(... band:)`: two regions with the same number of tracks each (the narrower side's fit, at most `maxColumns / 2`), the band as the middle gap, so no tile sits under the fold even when the grid is not centred on it. Track positions come from `DynamicGridMetrics.tracks` / `frame(column:span:)`; the measurement cache is keyed by width and band. **Lying flat there is no band** (owner, 2026-11-01): `windowHinge` is nil, the count stays even, and the tracks are even with no middle gap; the pose change animates because the shell changes the fold inside `withAnimation`. See [hinge-avoidance](hinge-avoidance.md).
- Camera grid: `minCellWidth` 160, `maxColumns` 4 (web `maxCellWidth={280} maxColumns={4}`).
- **One tile rule (owner, 2026-10-30; supersedes content-aware spans, four-is-a-floor and the per-grid minimums below where they differ).** Every tile grid (action and navigation tiles, room chips and room cards, Food and Food Spaces, Media, contact sensors, Quick Links, sheets) has the same minimum width, `DynamicGridMetrics.tileMinWidth` = 150 pt (scaled by the caller with `@ScaledMetric(relativeTo: .subheadline)` / `UKFont.scaled`, so AX sizes step down to fewer, wider tiles), and fits **at most four tiles in a row** (`DynamicGridMetrics.maxTileColumns`, enforced inside `DynamicGridMetrics.init` through `effectiveMaxColumns`, so a caller passing 5 or 6 still gets 4). The count is the most that fit at the minimum (4, then 3, then 2, then 1) and the tiles expand to share the row; tracks are equal, a short row is filled by shares (above), and no tile is widened to its text. Rationale (HIG Layout, Buttons, Typography): 150 pt holds the 44 pt target, a 30 pt glyph column, 12 pt insets and about 80 pt of title at the default size; text that does not fit wraps or runs as a ticker rather than taking a second track. Only grids of small controls (minimum under `controlMaxWidth` 100 pt: colour swatches, alarm mode buttons, weekday chips) keep their own caps, and two-up page sections (340 pt: the vacation dates beside the travellers) and option-row grids (300 pt presence choices) keep 2. Settings toggle tiles are tiles (owner, 2026-10-30: Household and Admin tiles follow the rule; `HouseholdTileGrid` / `UKHouseholdTiles.grid` default to it). Hinge rule unchanged: with a hinge the count is even (4 stays 4, 3 becomes 2 and the tiles expand) and a band gives equal tracks on both sides. Both apps: `DynamicGridLayout` (SwiftUI) and `UKDynamicGridView` / `UKGridSolver` (UIKit) call the same `DynamicGridMetrics`; consumers pass `DynamicGridMetrics.tileMinWidth` and `maxColumns: 4`. `TileMinimumWidth` and `DynamicGridMetrics.spans(...)` stay as pure helpers but no longer change a grid's spans; `gridSpan` still works (the weather and Chores quick-link tiles).
- **Content-aware spans (owner 2026-10-26, [action-tiles](action-tiles.md)); `UKGridSolver` still applies them to tiles that declare a `TileMinimumWidth` (Quick Links' Security System, the Air Purifier); `DynamicGridLayout` (SwiftUI) does not.** A tile sets `TileMinimumWidth` (a `LayoutValueKey`) from its content: the single-line width of its title and of the longest state it can show (`TileTextMeasure`), plus its disc slot. `DynamicGridMetrics.spans(minimums:trackWidth:columns:spacing:)` gives each tile `ceil(minimum / track)` tracks, capped at the column count, with an explicit `.gridSpan` as a floor; rows pack like the web's `fillRows: 'except-last'` (the last row keeps natural widths). A span never crosses the hinge band: the cap is that side's width. The measurement cache key includes a hash of the minimums. Live text never changes a minimum (the longest candidate is used), so a state update never re-spans the grid. iPad item tiles take at least 2 tracks (150 pt tracks). A tile that already spans its whole row or side turns an overflowing line into a ticker ([ticker](ticker.md)), keeping discs trailing down to the 96 pt text floor. Tests: `DynamicGridSpanTests`.
