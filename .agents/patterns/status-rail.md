# Status rail

> **What:** the horizontal chip row under a page title. **Read when:** adding a summary chip.

- Chips come from config (`HomeConfig.statusChips`) with an entity, symbol, tone and the sheet they open. Pages pass their own subset to `StatusRail(chips:idPrefix:)` (Security shows Security and Contact Sensors).
- `ChipRail` (HassDesign) lays the chips out: every width (iPhone, folded Duo, iPad, unfolded Duo) scrolls them sideways in one row across the whole page, over a hinge too (owner, 2026-10-26, replacing "regular width wraps" and the 24 pt hinge stop); only an accessibility Dynamic Type size wraps them with `FlowLayout` (`ChipRailMode.resolve(isAccessibilitySize:)`). A wrapped rail on a window with a hinge still stops 24 pt short of the fold (`ChipRailMetrics.trailingInset`, applied by `appliedInset(measured:hinge:mode:)` in `.wrap` only). Where the window is wide enough for every chip (iPad landscape, the unfolded Duo) the row simply fits and does not scroll. On the unfolded Duo's two-column pages (fold columns: Home, Security, the room pages) the rail sits above both columns, full page width, except at an accessibility size while partly folded: the wrapped rail is then the first block of the leading column, so the trailing column (Cameras on Home and Security) still starts at the top ([section-sides](../design/section-sides.md) rule 11, owner 2026-11-03). Home's `StatusRail`, Security's two chips and the room pages' `RoomStatusRail` all use it. Chips keep their own widths so live values never shift neighbors.
- Home has six chips in web order: Lights, Security, Climate, Occupancy, Contact Sensors, Air Quality. Summaries follow the web (`HomeConfig.summary`): Lights and Contact Sensors count their rooms' entities, Occupancy reads Detected/Clear, Air Quality joins the AQI and PM2.5 range helpers. Every chip but Security opens a [room overview sheet](room-overview-sheet.md); Security opens `SecuritySystemSheet`.
- Each chip is a button with the label "<title>, <summary>" for VoiceOver and opens a sheet.
- A chip narrower than its text wraps its title and state at word boundaries inside the capsule; it never truncates or shrinks its text (HIG Typography, Supporting Dynamic Type; section-sides verdict V1). A wrapped chip keeps the corner radius of a one-line chip at the current text size (`UKChipShape.cornerRadius`), in both chip views.
- Chips are pills (`StatusChipButton`, `StatusChipStyle`): a capsule at least 48 pt tall (web `.headerPill`). Owner, 2026-10-13: "Page-level chips are not pills like they are in web or the Apple Home app, and need background colors." Direction G ([direction-g](../design/direction-g.md), "Status chips follow the web"): a filled chip draws its fill with a slight top highlight, a white title, state in full white, and its glyph in the tint's glyph colour; an idle chip is neutral on the thin `module`.
- **One resolver.** `StatusChipAppearance` (HassUI/Features/Home) decides symbol, family, filled-or-neutral and the optional `ChipTint` for a Home/Security chip (`home(_:read:)`) and a room chip (`room(_:chip:)`); `StatusChipButton` only draws. A new chip rule goes there, with a test, never in a view.
- **Chip colours** (owner, 2026-10-16; sources in direction-g):
  - Lights on: amber with an orange glyph. Security: always its mode's fill (`ColorFamily.security(alarmState:)`), neutral while arming or unknown.
  - Climate: the temperature colour. Helper first (`input_text.demo_color_1`, a room's `…_climate_color`), else the web gradient at the range's mean (`ChipWebColor.climate`); a room's bed chip and a chip with no colour keep navy `#195482`. `ChipTint.fromWeb` scales it to 73% and darkens until white reads at 4.5:1, keeping the yellow thermometer where it holds 3:1.
  - Air Quality: the AQI colour on fill and glyph (`input_text.demo_color_2`, a room's `…_aqi_color`, else the web teal). Summary `AQI <range> · PM2.5 <range>` as on the web.
  - Occupancy: one `person` symbol, outline when clear, filled when detected; green fill only while detected, with no alarm or away tint (the web has none).
  - Contact Sensors: `ContactChipState` (HassCore/Chips), shared by Home, room rails and Security: all closed soft green, open at home amber `#8A5200`, open away coral `#B23A26`, open while armed red `#D70015` with an alert glyph; armed outranks away. Away is `ClimateLogic.isEffectivelyAway`.
- **Animation.** A fill or glyph colour change, and a chip turning filled or neutral, cross-fade (`StatusChipStyle.colorAnimation`: 0.6 s, 0.2 s under Reduce Motion). The animation is scoped with `.animation(_:body:)` to the colour modifiers only, so a longer state text never slides the capsule, and it is allowed under Reduce Motion because nothing moves.
- Room chips map their `RoomTone` to a family (`RoomTone.family`); `RoomController.chip(_:)` adds the contact state and web colour to `RoomChipDisplay` so the view layer needs no entity reads.
- **Chips start at the leading margin, always** (owner, 2026-10-17: "Security chips are not left-aligned. Even if all chips fit on screen, they still need to be left aligned."). Home's and room pages' rails only looked left-aligned because six chips overflow a phone. Cause: the compact rail hosts its chips in a `UIScrollView` (below) and sized the hosting view to the scroll view's width when the row was narrower; a hosting view centres its root, so Security's two chips drew centred. `HostedScrollView.layoutSubviews` now gives the hosted view exactly the row's natural width at the leading edge (`ChipRailMetrics.hostedContentFrame`: x = 0 left to right, the right-hand end of the content right to left, with the offset set to the row's start, `hostedLeadingOffset`), and `contentSize` stays at least the scroll view's width so nothing bounces. The wrapped rail (`FlowLayout`, regular width) places each row from the leading edge, mirrored in a right-to-left layout (`subviews.layoutDirection`). Rule: a row's alignment is the container's job, never a side effect of a hosting view's size; any new rail consumer inherits it. Journeys: `ChipAlignmentJourneyTests` (Security, Home and a room page: every row's first chip is `HassTokens.Spacing.l` from the page's leading edge, on iPhone, folded Duo, iPad portrait and landscape); unit tests: `ChipRailTests`.
- **Scrolling is UIKit's** (owner, 2026-10-16, iPhone 18 Pro Release: "horizontal scrolling on status chips in pages is stutter[y]"). The compact rail is a `UIScrollView` hosting the chips (`HostedHorizontalScroll`, handed the page's environment with `transformEnvironment(\.self)` because a hosting controller starts from a blank one), not a SwiftUI `ScrollView`. Cause: a `ScrollView` nested in a page's `ScrollView` writes its offset through the page's view graph on every frame (`ScrollViewCommitMutation.apply` → `AG::Graph::propagate_dirty`), and that work grows with the size and complexity of the page around it, not with the chips. Time Profiler on the simulator, 25 s of fast swipes (Home's rail over N glass tiles): 59 samples of it with no tiles, 247 with 24, 761 with 120. On the final code (120 tiles, iPhone 18 Pro simulator), SwiftUI `ScrollView` against the UIKit scroller: main-thread samples 6,020 → 2,943 (the rest is XCUITest's own accessibility snapshots), `ScrollViewCommitMutation.apply` 852 → 0, `_updateViewGraph` 1,907 → 60, `CA::Transaction::commit` 1,016 → 322. The chips were never re-evaluated during a swipe (no body calls), and neither the hinge measurement, the header fade, scroll telemetry nor Buttons were the cause (each removed alone changed nothing). Rules: keep a row that scrolls sideways inside a page out of SwiftUI's `ScrollView`; the other nested horizontal scrolls were measured in perf pass 2 ([performance](../testing/apple/performance.md), Round 2): the weather hourly carousel moved to the same view with paging (`PagedRow`, `HassDesign/PagedRow.swift`; main-thread time on the Weather sheet 1.1 s to 0.4 to 0.55 s per 8 s sweep, 0.37 s of which is the rain); Suggested Recipes stayed a SwiftUI `ScrollView` (60 image cards on a short page cost the same hosted, and hitched when a page was built); the feedback attachment strip sits in a short form and was left. The hinge inset is measured only by a wrapped rail in a window that has a hinge (`ChipRailMetrics.appliedInset`; a scrolling rail crosses the fold and measures nothing), because a window-space frame is recomputed on every frame the page scrolls.
- **Accessibility sizes wrap** (owner, 2026-10-24). `ChipRailMode.resolve(isAccessibilitySize:)` returns `.wrap` at an accessibility Dynamic Type size on any width (owner, 2026-10-26: this is now the only wrap): chips are nearly screen-wide there, so a sideways row would show one with the rest off screen and no cue. The state line under a chip's title is `.subheadline` (the value is the chip's content; `.caption` was the smallest text on the page), so the six-chip rail is about 4 pt taller.

- **Owned files** (also in `contracts/patterns.json`): `ChipRailArrangement.swift`, `ChipRailLayout.swift`.
