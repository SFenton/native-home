# Header edge fade: decision log

> **What:** how the header edge fade and the leading page title reached their current rules R1 to R7 in [header-edge-fade](../patterns/header-edge-fade.md). **Read when:** you want the reason behind R7's shape or are about to change how a title meets the bar; never to learn the rule.

> **Archived 2026-10-28.** The rule lives in the pattern doc; this keeps the full earlier write-up.

## 2026-10-13: the fade (R1 to R6)

Owner: "Headers should not have the translucent background behind them in pages or modals. Content should fade as it scrolls up to the headers. Check FestivalNativeApps for a good implementation." Ported from FestivalNativeApps `ModalTopEdgeFade` and its `scroll-edge` pattern R2 to R7.

## 2026-10-24: the title leads the bar (R7), full write-up

Owner, 2026-10-24: a collapsed title is left-aligned beside the Back chevron or menu button, not centred, and a title wider than the room between the bar's items scrolls like a ticker ([ticker](../patterns/ticker.md)) instead of being cut. `pageChrome()` applies `leadingBarTitle(_:)` (`HassDesign/LeadingCollapsedTitle.swift`) on every page: `.collapsing` where the large title sits below the bar (iPhone, iPad with the flyout; the bar title arrives once the large title has collapsed), `.inBar` where the title lives in the bar (Duo, compact iPad; `.inline` display mode, one size). It is a UIKit `titleView` that hosts the ticker and is wider than any title needs, so the bar gives it exactly the room between its leading and trailing items. A UIKit observer of the scroll view's top inset flips it on, so a scroll never updates SwiftUI's graph. **Not** a `.topBarLeading` item (the bar gives it almost no width; forced wide it overflows every header item into "...") and **not** a `.principal` item (not clamped to the room between items; 0 pt wide on iPad when flexible). The title view is set on the navigation item only while the bar title shows (at rest the bar is the system's own: the iPad bar stops widening a header button's hit area into the title region once any title view is set, and an empty one is announced by VoiceOver). It takes no touches, has an intrinsic height and is pinned with Auto Layout, and its hosting controller is not added as a child (UIKit throws). Reduce Motion: one line, cut with an ellipsis (`truncatesWhenStill`). VoiceOver: one header element named with the title. Known system behaviour kept: the large title below the bar truncates at rest, and the Duo hides the bar title once the page scrolls. Gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=shell-header` with `FENTON_GALLERY_PHONE=1` (large-title chrome), `FENTON_GALLERY_SCROLL=1`, `FENTON_GALLERY_SCROLL_TO=<row>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_TITLE=<text>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_PUSHED=1`. Mockup: [proposals/long-titles](../design/mockups/proposals/long-titles/long-titles.html).

Note for the next change to the fade: the bar's title area has gone through several rounds (centred title, `.principal`, `.topBarLeading`, a UIKit `titleView`); each earlier round failed on a system behaviour named above. Check this log before trying one again.
