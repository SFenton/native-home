# Food & Recipes: iPhone and iPad

> **What:** native Food implementation notes. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Food` or `HassCore/Recipes`.

- Service: `EverShelfRecipeService` (HassCore) over `HassConnection.callServiceWithResponse`, which sends websocket `call_service` with `return_response: true` and returns `response`. Parsing (`RecipeParsing`) ports `recipeTypes.ts`: snake or camel keys, loose numbers and flags, strict ingredient keys, bounded lists; an inconsistent group drops grouping rather than mis-grouping. Inventory reuses the transport.
- Routing: `FoodRoute` (`hub`, `recipes`, `inventory(InventorySpace)`) with `.foodNavigation()` on a `NavigationStack`; Home registers it, other lanes push `FoodRoute.hub`. Debug launch `FENTON_OPEN_FOOD=hub|recipes`. HIG: Navigation (push for hierarchy, sheet for a focused task).
- The space and All Food tiles open the Inventory lane's pages (`FoodInventoryScreen` → `InventoryDestinationScreen`, models from `inventoryModels` or the connection); `HomeRouteDestination.food` opens the hub (`FoodEntryScreen`).
- Hub tiles have their own action that sets the hub's `pushed` route (`.navigationDestination(item:)` → `FoodNavigation.destination`). **Decisions 2026-10-16 (owner: "All Food should be a tile", "Food Spaces / All Food should have colours like the web"):** All Recipes (full width), All Food (one grid cell wide, as the web's `singleTileGrid`) and the five spaces are all `GlassTile(kind: .navigate, isActive: true)` filled in the web's tile colours as the Food `ColorFamily`s (`.recipes`, `.food`, `.pantry`, `.fridge`, `.freezer`, `.spiceRack`, `.cabinet`; table in [direction-g](../../design/direction-g.md#colour)), white glyph and ink, equal-height grid. They are always filled: the colour tells the places apart, not an on state. `FoodLinkRow` is gone. "Suggested Recipes" is a `PageSectionHeader`, drawn by the carousel; **owner, 2026-10-30: no "Page 1 of 5" text beside it and no "{n} Items • {N} Expiring Soon" subtitle on the All Food and space tiles** (both apps; the page control keeps its "Page 2 of 5" accessibility value; the inventory page drops its "41 Items • 3 Expiring Soon" line too). A tile drawn with `.allowsHitTesting(false)` inside a `NavigationLink` label ignored every tap on iPhone ([entity-tile](../../patterns/entity-tile.md) on the Rooms branch). `testHubTileOpens{AllRecipes,Fridge,AllFood}` taps them; `testHubTilesAreColouredTiles` checks they are buttons with their count lines.
 Loading (owner, 2026-10-16: "graceful spinner fade-in → content loads → spinner fade-out → content fade-in"): the hub, Recipes and the inventory pages sit in `ContentGate(timing: .page, reloadTiming: .results)` ([content-gate](../../patterns/content-gate.md)), the web's `DashboardPageLoadGate` timings (`ha-sfenton-react-dash@9ac2a46:src/components/shell/DashboardPageLoadGate.tsx`, `src/constants/loading.ts`: 1 s minimum, 500 ms spinner exit, 170 ms content enter, 10 s give-up). The hub is ready once Suggested Recipes settled (web `settled={carouselLoadState !== 'loading'}`, `FoodHubPage.tsx:34-39`); Recipes once the first page loaded or failed, with the floating actions outside the gate so a search keeps its field. A reopen with the data already there shows at once.
- Hub: one column; two (suggestions + All Recipes, All Food + spaces) where `SplitPolicy` allows. The carousel is a paged `ScrollView` with page dots VoiceOver can adjust. HIG: Collections, Page controls.
- Recipes: "Sorted by Ingredients Available" under the large title (`RecipeGrid.sortSummary`), then `RecipeGrid` on `DynamicGridMetrics` (160 pt min, 2 to 8 columns) of `ImageCard`s; shared floating actions and `FilterSheet` ([floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md)). Results and hydration are announced. HIG: Searching, Sheets.
- Primary buttons (Retry, Open in Cookidoo on Instructions, Add to Grocery List) are G's `PrimaryButton`/`PrimaryButtonStyle` (`#0071E3`), one per screen; Open Source Recipe on General is a list row in the action tint (`RecipeLinkRow`). `FoodStyle.action` is gone.
- Accessibility sizes: `ImageCard` becomes a row (72 pt thumbnail beside the title); fact rows drop the glyph and put the value under the label (`RecipeFactRow`); the filter's horizon becomes a menu. HIG: Typography (prioritize text at large sizes).
- The split pane has its own header (title, Close) instead of a `NavigationStack`: a stack nested in the Food stack's Recipes page showed nothing on iPad landscape. The hub keeps the open recipe's `RecipeDetailModel` in `@State`; built in the sheet closure, live entity updates rebuilt it and reset the tab. The hub carousel clips its pages in split columns (unclipped, page two drew under the inventory column).
- Debug: `FENTON_FOOD_FIXTURES=1` gives the signed-in app's Food pages the fixture service; `FENTON_FOOD_DEBUG=1` shows the open detail model's identity and tab (`recipe.debug`) for journeys. They separated live data from the app when the iPad sheet ignored taps (cause: the hero photo's hit area, see image-card).
- Detail: a `.large` sheet, explicitly `presentationDetents([.large])` after `hassSheet(.page)` (owner, 2026-10-16: other sheets size to their content but a recipe stays full screen; `testRecipeIsFullScreenAndInstructionsAreCentred`), with the circle-x Close on single-page layouts, "8/9 ingredients" as the iOS 26 subtitle (`sheetSubtitle`); where a split is allowed (iPad landscape, unfolded Duo landscape) the trailing half shows it with Close, the open card is outlined and marked selected, and folding moves it into the sheet. Tabs are the bottom glass `modalTabBar` (`recipe.tabs`). Facts, ingredient groups, equipment and steps are `ModuleList`s (`#2C2C2E` cells under `modalChrome()`; the split pane sets `tileSurface` to `.sheet`; glyph-less lists pass `hasGlyphs: false`; facts are `ModuleRow`s; chips wrap in HassDesign's `FlowLayout`) under 15 Semibold secondary headers (`RecipeSectionHeader`); ingredient marks are a green check, a question mark (uncertain match) or an empty circle. HIG: Segmented controls, Split views.
- **Grocery button (owner, 2026-10-16):** the web's "Add Missing Ingredients to Groceries" is "Add to Grocery List" (`GroceryAction.title`) with "Adds the missing ingredients for this recipe to your shopping list." under it (`GroceryAction.explanation`, also its accessibility hint). A recipe with nothing to add keeps the disabled reason "No missing ingredients to add." in that place; the done state reads "Added to Grocery List". The service calls and idempotency are unchanged.
- **Instructions are centred (owner, 2026-10-16):** the detail's scroll content has `minHeight` equal to the sheet's visible height on the Instructions tab and centres, so steps that fit sit in the vertical middle and longer ones scroll from the top (`scrollBounceBehavior(.basedOnSize)`).
- Symbols: Food's MDI names live in `SymbolMap` with the rest (a duplicate key in its dictionary literal crashes at launch, so grep before adding).
- Gallery (dark, on the house backdrop): `FENTON_GALLERY=food`, `FENTON_GALLERY_ROUTE=hub|recipes`, `FENTON_GALLERY_FOOD_MODE=error|empty|slow`; 120 fixture recipes (every fourth Cookidoo), recorded calls in `gallery.lastCall`.
  The debug "Last call" line sits bottom-leading in a 180 pt chip (a full-width bar covered the floating actions; a top inset covered the back button) and is hidden at accessibility sizes (it clipped and covered the page). Fixture sorts break ties by id, so by availability the grid leads with 1, 6, 11; journeys open 6 and check 11 beside the pane.
- Tests: `RecipeParsingTests`, `RecipeGroceryPolicyTests`, `RecipeFormattingTests`, `RecipeServiceTests`, `FoodConfigTests` (HassCore); `FoodModelTests`, `FoodViewTests`, `FoodStateTests` (HassUI; scripted service for failures, paging, hydration and grocery outcomes; `RecipesScreen`/`FoodHubScreen` take initial `WindowMetrics` and selection so hosted renders reach the split); the `Food*JourneyTests` classes over `FoodJourneyTestCase` (devices, audits, screenshots; Hub, Suggestions, Recipes, Recipe sheets, Recipe detail, Wide layout and Live). Sources by area: `RecipesScreen` (page, results, selection), `RecipeGrid`, `RecipeSortFilterSheets`; models `FoodModels` (container), `SuggestedRecipesModel`, `RecipeFilters`, `RecipeCollectionModel`, `RecipeDetailModel`.

## Columns (UIKit port, owner 2026-11-01)

A Food space tile on the hub (All Food, Pantry, Fridge, Freezer, Spice Rack, Cabinet) is `ShellRoute.foodSpace(rawValue)`. In a window that splits (iPad landscape, unfolded Duo landscape) the hub is column one, one column wide (recipes, then the spaces), and the space opens as column two with Close in the section bar's column group; "Select a food space to see its items" fills it until one is chosen, and another tile replaces the open space. Elsewhere the space is pushed. The Inventory page in a half-width column opens its item editor as a sheet. All Recipes still pushes full width. Without a shell (the gallery) the hub keeps its two-pane form and pushes. See [split-view](../../design/apple/split-view.md), "Column navigation".
