# Rooms: iPhone and iPad

> **What:** native room page notes. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Rooms` or `HassCore/Rooms`.

- Routing: `HomeRoute` (`Features/Home/HomeRoute.swift`) is the one registry for pages pushed under Home: rooms plus Media, Custom Lights, Sprinklers, Vacuums (`VacuumsScreen`, placeholder without a connection), Food and Grocery List. Lanes replace their placeholder in `HomeRouteDestination`; never add a second mechanism. Push with `.homeRouteDestination($route)` (`navigationDestination(item:)`), which works in plain and path-driven stacks.
- `RoomController` (HassCore) owns reads, sheet routing and service calls, so they are unit-tested without views; it shares one `MediaRemoteController` per remote with the page's remote sheet and app tiles.
- Quick Links is a toolbar button (HIG: Toolbars) on every room and, in compact width before iOS 26, on section roots; in regular width (iPad, unfolded Duo) section roots show the floating glass circle instead (`.agents/design/apple/ipados.md`). The sheet holds its own `NavigationStack` (Rooms and Security System push with Back); page links push after the sheet dismisses so the push never races the dismissal.
- Single page in every pose; the section grid goes to two columns when the window is wide (`DynamicGridLayout(minCellWidth: 340, maxColumns: 2)`). A Rooms list beside the room in landscape waits for the shell's on-demand split.
- Device sheets: `RoomDeviceModal` (`Features/Rooms/RoomDeviceRouting.swift`) opens the lanes' own sheets outside `RoomCardSheet` (they carry their own stack): bathroom fan (`BathroomFanSheet`, `.hassSheet(.form)`), air quality (`AirPurifierSheet`, form), humidifier (`HumidifierSheet`, `.page`), grill (`GrillSheet`, form), vacuum (`VacuumSheet`, `.hassSheet(.page)`), and the Primary Bedroom's wake light (`WakeLightSheet`) and beds (`BedSideSheet`) on one page-wide `BedroomDevices` with `.hassSheet(.page)`; the wake light's "open this bed" queues the bed on Alarms until its sheet closes. `canEdit` follows the signed-in user's admin flag (false until known); callers that can't read service responses keep the fallback. A config miss, or the vacuum and humidifier without the app's connection (the gallery), keeps the generic `RoomDeviceFallback` tiles.
- The bathroom fan tile (`BathroomFanRoomTile`) has Lock and Power action discs (`room.card.Fan.action.lock|power`, 44 pt toggles, [action-tiles](../../patterns/action-tiles.md)) and shares one `BathroomFanController` per fan with its sheet (web `BathroomFanCommandProvider`); both send the fan script's explicit target, never a toggle.
- Hook points still open: `RoomCardView` (wake light, dishwasher tiles) and `RoomDeviceModal.resolve` (dishwasher). Garage cards draw the Security `GarageDoorTile` and Quick Links' Security System detail shows `SecurityControls`.
- Accessibility: identifiers `room.card.<title>`, `room.chip.<title>`, `room.sheet.<sheet>`, `room.section.<title>`, `quickLinks.<id>`, `rooms.<path>`. Read-only tiles are `.state` tiles (not buttons). The Rooms grid draws each room as a coloured tile (`GlassTile(fillRGB: RoomPalette.fill(forRoom:))`: a solid fill, filled white glyph and white text; direction-g Colour: Room tiles). It ranks by the visit counters the web uses (see the audit below). Quick Links and room-grid tiles are `GlassTile`s with their own action (in-sheet pages push through the sheet's `NavigationStack` path); never wrap a tile drawn with `.allowsHitTesting(false)` in a `Button` or `NavigationLink`, because the outer control then gets no taps (seen on iPhone, 2026-10-11). Occupancy reads "Hidden" under `FENTON_REDACT_PRIVATE`.
- Gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=rooms` (fixture store, recording caller, last call shown), `FENTON_GALLERY_ROOM=<path>` opens a room, `FENTON_GALLERY_QUICK_LINKS=1` opens Quick Links.
- **Most-visited ordering audit** (owner question, 2026-10-16: are room tiles, and other modals the web sorts by access, ranked by most visited?). The web ranks exactly one list by access: the Rooms grid (`RoomNavigationGrid.tsx` with `roomAccessRanking.ts`, count `Number(counter.demo_media_room_<room>.state)`, ties in configured order); a search of the web source for counters, ranking, "recent", "frequent" and `localStorage` found nothing else (localStorage only holds a weather briefing, a viewport cache and diagnostics), and `RoomNavigationGrid` is used only by Quick Links. Native already matched: `RoomNavigation.ranked` reads the same counters through `EntityStore`, so the order follows the websocket and the disk snapshot, and opening a room from the grid calls `script.demo_script_2` with `variables.room` (the counters live in Home Assistant, so the count persists across launches and devices). `RoomsTests.theGridReRanksAsVisitCountersChange` proves the order changes as counters arrive and survives a snapshot restore. The Climate Advanced Controls rooms, room overview sheets and chip sheets are not ranked on the web, so they keep configured order.
- Journeys: `RoomsJourneyTestCase` area classes (page, fan, Quick Links, wide layout, capture); see [testing](../../testing/apple/README.md).
- **Content-aware tiles (owner, 2026-11-01).** The Living Room's Air Purifier takes its section's full width on iPad (title, state, `power` disc and chevron do not fit half of it); the Climate and Devices sections still sit side by side. The Quick Links sheet scrolls on the folded Duo because Security System takes a row. See [action-tiles](../../patterns/action-tiles.md).
