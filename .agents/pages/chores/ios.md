# Chores, Groceries and To-Do (iPhone and iPad)

> **What:** native structure and departures for the todo pages. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Chores` or `HassCore/Todo`.

- `ChoresScreen` (tab) and `AdminTodoScreen` (Settings row, hidden from non-admin viewers by the cached viewer id) host a `ChoresModel` (service, shared `TodoStore`, viewer id cached in `UserDefaults` `chores.viewerUserId` and refreshed from `auth/current_user`). Sub-pages push with `navigationDestination(for: ChoresConfig.Page.self)`, which hands the `ChoresModel` on explicitly: a pushed page's environment comes from the section's `NavigationStack`, above `ChoresModelHost`, and without it every quick link crashed (2026-10-13).
- `TodoStore.shared(for:)` keeps one `TodoListController` per list for the connection, so lists keep their items across pages, and `TodoItemsCache` (Application Support `TodoItems.json`, file-protected, per server) shows the last items on a cold launch before the first fetch. Sign-out clears it and the viewer id.
- Every visible list loads from a zero-size background view, drawn or not, so a hidden list reappears when its entity count rises.
- Add is the shared floating action bar ([floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md)); HIG: Buttons. Sheets are `NavigationStack` + `Form` with Cancel / confirm in the bar (HIG: Sheets); the Choreapp form opens at the large detent, the one-field sheets at medium. The add sheet shows Task Name and Description as two sections, each with a `FormHeader` and a `FormFooter` saying what goes in it, and no "Choreapp" subtitle (owner, 2026-10-16).
- Rows (owner, 2026-10-13; updated 2026-10-16): no card behind a row. The check circle is **the size of the title** (owner, 2026-10-16: it was bigger): it takes `.font(.body)` at the symbol's default scale (it had `.imageScale(.large)`) on a center-aligned `HStack`, so it follows Dynamic Type with the title and centers vertically on the whole text block, a wrapped title and due line included (owner, 2026-10-28, #197: it sat on the first line), inside a 24 pt `@ScaledMetric` column that keeps the title on the module's hairline inset. The hit target is the whole row (52 pt, 60 pt with a due line), far over 44 pt. The edit glyph is `square.and.pencil` labelled "Edit {title}" on every page (it was `info.circle`; HIG SF Symbols).
- **The page is a `ScrollView` of a `VStack` on iOS 18 and later, and the `List` on iOS 17** (the swipe row's pan recognizer needs iOS 18, and a SwiftUI `DragGesture` row stopped the page scrolling, 2026-10-28; `TodoPageView.pageScroller`) (owner, 2026-10-27, asked for a `LazyVStack`; the A/B below moved it to a plain `VStack`: "Change Settings to VStack and Chores to LazyVStack and measure"; it was an inset-grouped `List` from 2026-10-16). A profile of the long house showed 55% of the main thread while scrolling in the `List` collection view creating and self-sizing a cell for every row that entered the viewport (3 to 4 ms each, redone on every pass); removing row chrome moved it 5 to 10%. Measured: the `LazyVStack` cost the phone 60% more main-thread CPU than the `List` and hitched (pass 2 as costly as pass 1: a per-frame cost of the lazy stack itself, 20 ms worst turns as rows built); a plain `VStack` that builds every row when the page opens (tens of rows) had no turn over 8 ms on the long-house fixture and cost 0.3 s more to open (simulator, 2026-10-27). `TodoPageView` lays out the quick links, then one `TodoStackSection` per list (a `Group` body of the page's `VStack`; named `TodoLazySection` until 2026-10-29) inside a 16 pt side margin (`TodoPageLayout.horizontalMargin`, where the list's own inset put the module's edges). Each task row is a `SwipeActionRow` ([swipe-row](../../patterns/swipe-row.md)) that draws its share of the module with `ModuleRowBackground` (dense when the section has an overdue row) and a hairline from `moduleRowSeparator` (`HassDesign/ModuleList.swift`), behind the sliding content. The empty state is the shared `StateMessage` overlay centred in the page ([state-message](../../patterns/state-message.md)). One implicit `motionSafeAnimation` on the page, keyed by `TodoPageLayout.animationKey`, animates a row away when it is completed or deleted and back in on rollback (fade under Reduce Motion). `TodoListActions` is the one source for the check-off, Undo, delete, the swipe buttons and the context menu, for this layout and the `List` section the Summary sheet's chore tabs still use (`TodoListSection`, a sheet cannot take the lazy page). The experiment switch that restored the `List` and the `LazyVStack` (`FENTON_LEGACY_LIST`) was removed after the A/B (2026-10-28). Due dates parse once (`TodoItem.DueDateCache`).
- **The quick links are the list's first row (owner, 2026-10-26; replaces the 2026-10-16 section header).** A row of several `NavigationLink`s activates every one of them on any tap, so the tiles are `Button`s that set a `navigationDestination(item:)` state on `ChoresContent` (the item destination sits outside the list; the 2026-10-16 hang was the header fade recomputing and no longer applies). The header is a plain row (`TodoPlainRow`, zero insets), so it lines up with the headings and modules through the list's own insets; `AlignedToListMargins` and its negative padding are gone. `ChoresListLayoutJourneyTests.testQuickLinksAlignWithTheListMargins` asserts the tiles start and end where the headings and modules do. **The first row of any list page starts `TodoPageLayout.leadingRowTopInset` (12 pt) below the top** (owner, 2026-10-26: the Q of "Quick Links" was cut): an inset-grouped list masks its first row to the group's rounded top corners even with a clear background, so a first row with a zero top inset loses the corner of its first glyph. The Chores header row, every page's leading section heading and the Summary's Expired Food heading all use the one constant; `ChoresListLayoutJourneyTests.testQuickLinksHeadingClearsTheListsTopCorners` asserts the gap.
- **Swipe design** (owner, 2026-10-16; implemented by `SwipeActionRow` since 2026-10-27 on iOS 18 and later, the system's `swipeActions` on the iOS 17 `List` only (iOS 27's `swipeActions` in a stack was tried and dropped, [swipe-row](../../patterns/swipe-row.md)); HIG, Lists and tables: the row's primary action leads, secondary and destructive actions trail, as in Mail and Reminders): leading swipe (left to right in LTR) is **Complete** (or Mark Not Done), green, full swipe performs it; trailing swipe is **Delete** (outermost, red, full swipe asks "Delete {title}?" through a confirmation dialog because the removal is irreversible) then **Edit** (blue). SwiftUI's `.leading` and `.trailing` edges mirror in right-to-left layouts, so the order flips with the language. Every swipe action is also in the row's context menu (destructive last, HIG Context menus) and is a VoiceOver custom action on the row's toggle (Edit, Delete; Complete is the toggle itself). `TodoRowActions` is the one source for all three, so they cannot disagree. Fills are the darker app fills (green `#2A732E`, blue `#0071E3`, red `#D93025`) so the white label keeps 4.5:1. Complete goes through `TodoListController.toggle` (its optimistic statuses, the todo list's own revert timer, rather than `OptimisticActions`, which tracks single entity states); Delete is `TodoListController.remove` (row leaves at once, returns with the error on failure).
- **The Chores tab badge** (`OverdueChoresBadge`, applied in `MainTabView`) publishes the viewer's overdue count, the open items of their `…_past_due_with_unassigned` list, to `ShellNavigator.overdueChores` (web `BADGED_NAV_PATH`). Profile, Your Summary, the Chores tab and the resident pages read the same lists.
- **Quick-link tiles** (owner, 2026-10-16): filled tiles with a white filled glyph, one colour per page from `ColorFamily` (the two resident chore cases: ultramarine `#0022CC` and magenta `#B410AE`; then `choreUnassigned` raspberry `#C2127A`, `choreHome` violet `#6600CC`, `groceries` chestnut `#802200`; a link keeps its colour whoever is signed in). The web's chore colours (`ha-sfenton-react-dash@f5a7bf9:src/constants/portedDashboard.ts:198-204`: groceries rust `#9B4348`, first resident teal `#006078`, second resident orange `#D46C00`, Unassigned purple `#513A7E`, Home teal `#408F9A`) collide with device families (cooling and air teal, heating orange, cleaning purple, media pink and away red), so they are not used; the new fills are each at least 0.05 apart in OKLab from every device, security and Food fill and every room fill (`ColorFamilyTests.choreTilesUseColoursNothingElseDoes`; a first set of indigo, fuchsia, mustard, slate and cocoa was dropped when the Food and room palettes landed on the same hues) and keep 4.5:1 for the white title, the 92% subtitle and the status-chip lift.
- Departures: quick links use the glass tile face with the web color on the icon and border rather than a 72% color fill (text contrast on the material, HIG: Materials); due tones put the color on a symbol and keep label-colored text (4.5:1 on the material); due date and time are optional toggles with native pickers instead of empty date fields; Specific Days of Week is a checkmark list instead of a multi-select box.
- **Columns (UIKit port, owner 2026-11-01).** Quick-link pages (Your Chores, the other resident's, Unassigned, Home Tasks, Groceries) are `ShellRoute.chores(pageId)`: in a window that splits (iPad landscape, unfolded Duo landscape, `SplitPolicy`) the Chores list is column one and the page opens as column two with Close in the section bar's column group and its title as content; elsewhere it is pushed. Add and edit open their form as the next column (title as content, Save and Close in the column group, Close asks before dropping edits and waits for a save); a todo page that is itself a column opens its forms as sheets. See [split-view](../../design/apple/split-view.md), "Column navigation".
- Debug gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=chores` (+ `FENTON_GALLERY_TODO_PAGE=<route>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_VIEWER=<resident>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_EMPTY=1`) with a fixture service that applies check-offs, adds, renames and deletes.
