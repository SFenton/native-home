# Vacuums: iPhone and iPad

> **What:** native Vacuums implementation notes. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Vacuums` or `HassCore/Vacuums`.

- `VacuumsScreen` reads the environment `HassConnection`; `VacuumsPage` takes a store and caller so the gallery and hosted tests drive it with fixtures. A summary line under the large title ("1 Cleaning · 1 Needs Attention", `VacuumPageFormat`), then Robot Vacuums as `.cleaning` tiles on `DynamicGridLayout` (filled while working, off when docked, the error face with the issue appended) and Auto-Clean as a `ModuleList` of switch `ModuleRow`s, both under `PageSectionHeader`s. Where `SplitPolicy.allowsSplit` (unfolded Duo landscape, iPad landscape) they sit side by side, each scrolling on its own; everywhere else they stack. HIG: Layout, Split views.
- Auto-Clean on the page is a list of switch rows (`VacuumAutoCleanController`): the Main Floor pause switch is inverted (enabled when off). Optimistic, cleared on confirmation, reverted after 8 s. Inside the vacuum sheet the Auto-Clean **section** is room tiles with a check disc (checked = included), not switches and not checkmark rows ([vacuum-sheet-redesign](../../design/vacuum-sheet-redesign.md)).
- Tapping a robot opens `VacuumSheet` (`.hassSheet(.page)`, large detent, page sizing on iPad); see [vacuum-sheet](../../patterns/vacuum-sheet.md). The page opens from Quick Links (`HomeRoute.vacuums`); room vacuum cards open the same sheet.
- The map is the Mapped map camera's `entity_picture` PNG with the map JSON in its `zTXt` chunk; see [mapped-map](../../patterns/valetudo-map.md). It polls every 3.5 s while the robot is live; an unavailable robot shows its last reported map without paths.
- Services come from `get_services` once per sheet, so scripts the server lacks (area clean, dock) disable their buttons instead of failing.
- Debug gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=vacuums` with `FENTON_GALLERY_VACUUM=<map id>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_TAB=controls|zones|autoClean|more|info` and `FENTON_GALLERY_SCENARIO=docked|cleaning|error|resumable|unavailable`. Fixture maps and a recording caller; nothing reaches a real robot. Journeys: `VacuumJourneyTestCase` and its area classes.
- Area editor gestures route through `MapAreaEdit` (HassCore) and the `ValetudoMapScene` editing helpers, so draw, move, resize, pan, pinch and the VoiceOver actions are unit tested. Page scrolling is gated on a `@GestureState`, not the drag's job: a cancelled drag never calls `onEnded`, and gating on the job left the page unscrollable.
- Journeys (`VacuumJourneyTestCase` and its area classes, see [testing](../../testing/apple/README.md)): the section bar (`vacuum.tabs`) floats at the bottom, so a sheet counts as presented once Close is hittable, and `scrollToTabContent` scrolls by the panel (`vacuum.tabContent`). Room and Auto-Clean tiles are **selection tiles** (`VacuumChoiceTileView`: a `GlassTile` with one `.choice` disc, `TileActionItem.indicator`), not toggles or system switches, so there is no On/Off or 1/0 value. A chosen tile carries the `isSelected` trait. A room reads label "<Room>" ("<Room>, cleaning order N" when chosen) with value "Selected" / "Not selected"; it is unavailable (not tappable) while the robot is busy. An Auto-Clean room reads label "<Room> auto-clean" with value "Included", "Skipped" or "Unavailable" (a chosen disc means included; a skipped room's subtitle says Skipped). Identifiers: `vacuum.room.<entity>` and `vacuum.autoCleanRoom.<entity>`. There are **no `vacuum.target` segments** any more: Use Area / Use Rooms are action tiles, and settings (Mode, Fan, Water, Passes) are **pop-up rows** (the Mode row's value reads "Vacuum and Mop" even when `input_text.*_vacuum_mode` says "Vacuum & Mop": a recorded departure from the web, owner 2026-10-29): tap the row's menu button, then the option; the Water row is absent in Vacuum mode and Fan in Mop mode (wait for the 0.3 s row animation before asserting). Status is three read-only tiles (Status, Battery, Dock). Pause, Stop and the dock commands are action tiles. `reveal` treats a control under the gallery's Last call bar as not yet reachable, and never starts a drag at the screen's bottom edge (the Home gesture backgrounds the app and the audit then fails with "Invalid target app"). In the area editor, scroll from below the map: a drag on the map moves the area.
