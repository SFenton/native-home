# State-styled tile (component)
> **What:** the one tile for doors, locks, blinds, the alarm, robots and sensors, with a class and a style per state. **Read when:** you build or change the tile, its transition component or a built-in style.
- Mockup: [state-styled-tile.html](state-styled-tile.html) · status shipped
- Source: `apple/Sources/HassCore/TileActions/StateStyle.swift`, `StateStyle+BuiltIn.swift`, `StateStyleActions.swift`, `apple/Sources/HassDesign/TileActions/StateTileLook.swift`, `apple/Sources/HassUI/UIKitPort/Design/UKStateTileTransition.swift`, `UKTileActions.swift`, `UKGlassTileFaceView.swift`, `UKArcSpinnerView.swift` · Spec: `.agents/design/state-styled-tile.md`, `.agents/design/state-styles.md` · Patterns: action-tiles, entity-tile, optimistic-actions, motion
- Tile contract states: swipe to adjust (at rest, mid-swipe, the ends, band off, right to left, AX5), tile ink on a page and in dark and light sheets (primary warning state line, grey unavailable tiles). State words do not apply: the state text is authored per state
- States covered: the five classes and the active flag, storyboard (closed, opening, closing, open, error), built-in styles, request phase in the app and through a Home Assistant script, Reduce Motion, offline, long text, ax5
- Motion: a swipe on a cover (the face and state line stay), per-layer continuity (one arc through Opening and Closing), the disc and arc cross-fade, the garage sequence, the 1 s flap guard, the warning pulse, the error wiggle (bounce on iOS 17), breathe
- Devices: iPhone, Duo folded, Duo unfolded portrait and landscape, iPad portrait and landscape
- Open notes: the contract states follow the hosted stills in `build/evidence/screens/native-tilecontract-fba79957`; the warning face in a light sheet is not drawn (no evidence); helpers in `components/tiles/_tile-contract.js` (promote to kit). The arc replaces the disc while a tap does nothing (option D: `proposals/state-tile-indicator`). Symbol effect timings are the system's and are approximated. The builder is the kit's `StateTile` (`_kit/components/StateTile.js`). The look differs from the fixed shapes in two places, listed in `.agents/design/state-styles.md`.
