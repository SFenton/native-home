Vacuum sheet redesign: names off the map, equal status tiles, one grammar for settings

The owner flagged three things in the vacuum sheet: room names on the map, uneven status chips, and mixed pop-up and segmented settings. A pass over every section found more of the same kind. This page draws the design the owner chose, at live size.

Sheets: Every frame and crop below is drawn with the kit's components; it follows the page theme in Auto.

Decided 2026-10-28. The owner picked option B, the Recommended column, with amendments (no Locate tile, Rooms keeps its own section, tiles for every action, selection tiles in Rooms and Auto-Clean, Use Area and Use Rooms, an animated Water row). The final design is below; the options that were weighed are folded away at the end as the record.

Options considered (the proposal as reviewed)

1. Cause: what the owner reported, and what the deep pass found

1. Room names on the map are a non-starter

The map draws every room name, caption2 semibold, and lets it spill past a small room (the 2026-10-24 owner rule "Room names are always drawn"). On a 300 x 360 map with 11 rooms the names collide with badges and each other.

ValetudoMapView.swift:344 drawNames; patterns/valetudo-map.md:22

2. Status chips are uneven widths

Status, Battery and Dock are pills in a FlowLayout, so each is as wide as its value ("Returning", "Dock Washing") and they wrap raggedly. The web uses a two-column grid of equal pills, with Locate as the fourth.

VacuumMapPanel.swift:197 FlowLayout of VacuumStatusChip; web ha-sfenton-react-dash@11cdab2:src/components/hass/VacuumCard.tsx:1266 (DynamicGrid columns=2: Status, Battery, Dock Status, Locate pill)

3. Settings mix pop-ups and segmented controls

Suction (5 options) is a pop-up; Passes is a segmented control beside its title; Mode and Water are segmented under their titles; Rooms/Area is another segmented control. Four grammars in one list.

VacuumComponents.swift:314 VacuumSettingRow (2-4 options segmented, 5+ menu); VacuumTabs.swift:170 Rooms/Area segmented

What else the deep pass found

  • State is shown three times while cleaning. The sheet subtitle ("Cleaning · 74%"), the Status chip, and the run card's own header ("Cleaning") say the same thing. VacuumSheet.swift:195 subtitle; VacuumMapPanel.swift:197; VacuumTabs.swift:147 runtimeCard header
  • Auto-Clean checkmarks are inverted. A tick means the room is skipped; everywhere else in the app a tick or a switch means included or on. VacuumTabs.swift:362-384
  • Five sections force symbol-only tabs. At compact width the bottom bar shows icons only (ModalTabBar.swift:17, owner 2026-10-16); its own doc comment says fewer than five read as text, which the code does not do either.
  • "Selected Rooms" jumps to another tab. The chips row on Controls is a button that switches to the Rooms section, so choosing rooms means leaving the screen that has the Clean button. VacuumTabs.swift:220-252 onOpenRooms
  • Locate is a glass button over the map. It floats on the picture, a different grammar from every other command on the sheet, and the web has it in the status grid. VacuumMapPanel.swift:120
  • Mockup versus code (noted, not changed): the atlas mockup draws a name only when it fits; the code draws all of them and lets them spill. Today is drawn here as the code does it.

2. Today, recommended and alternative, area by area

Fixture data is the atlas's: Main Floor (11 rooms), Kitchen, Living Room and Office selected in that order, Guest Room skipped by Auto-Clean. Options: Today Recommended Alternative

a. Map: names, no names, or a glyph per room

Recommended: no text on the map. A selected room is white with its numbered badge, a skipped room is hatched, the rest are flat. Alternative: a room SF Symbol per room; Closet and Hallway share a wardrobe glyph and the two bathrooms share a shower, so the glyph alone is ambiguous. Performance: dropping names removes a per-draw Text resolve and measure for every room.

b. Status: equal tiles instead of ragged chips

Recommended: a 2 x 2 grid of equal tiles (caption over value, toned glyph): Status, Battery, Dock, and Locate as a button tile, which moves the glass Locate off the map and matches the web. At accessibility sizes the grid is one column. Alternative: one 3-up equal strip in a single card; Locate stays on the map. Shown with the long values "Returning" and "Dock Washing".

c. Cleaning settings: one grammar

Recommended: every select is a pop-up row, title left, value and chevron.up.chevron.down trailing in action blue, for Mode, Suction, Water and Passes under one "Cleaning Settings" header (direction-g "Choosing one option" already allows pop-ups for suction and passes; the web uses selects for all). The open menu is drawn on Suction. Alternative: all segmented under the title; five suction segments are tall and break at accessibility sizes. Today's row title is "Fan" in the code; the brief calls it Suction (see open questions). The long description lines under today's rows are dropped in the recommended rows to keep them 52 pt; they stay as the VoiceOver hint.

d. Section structure: five sections or four

Recommended: four sections. The Rooms section merges into a single Clean section (map, status grid, room cards at 76 pt minimum with order badges, a "Draw Area..." row, Cleaning Settings, one blue Clean button), then Auto-Clean, Dock, Info. The Rooms/Area segmented control and the "Selected Rooms" jump go; with four sections the bar can show text labels. Alternative: keep five sections and only replace the Rooms/Area segmented control with a Selected Rooms row plus a Draw Area row. The frames are tall (the real sheet scrolls) so the whole section shows.

Area drawn

With an area drawn, the room grid is replaced by one Area row with Edit and Clear. Clear returns the grid. Choosing Draw Area... while rooms are selected raises the existing "Switch to Area?" alert (see h).

The bottom bar

Two columns: iPad landscape and Duo unfolded landscape

Where the window can split (SplitPolicy), the map and status take the left column and the Clean section the right. The whole sheet is shown at the window's real size; the sheet's own scroll covers what does not fit.

e. Cleaning (minimal) state

While a run is going the map grows and the setup collapses. Recommended: the run card drops its state header (the subtitle and the Status tile already say it); Pause and Stop stay. A third crop shows the paused state, where Resume is the one primary button.

f. Auto-Clean: switches, on means included

Today a checkmark means skipped. Recommended: a switch row per room, on = included, the subtitle reads "Skipped" when off, and the map hatches skipped rooms (precedent: the Main Floor pause switch on the Vacuums page is already inverted for the same reason). First seven of eleven rooms are shown.

g. Dock and Info

Mostly unchanged: blue-title action rows in Dock, trailing values in Info (orange soon, red due). The one change: while the dock washes the mop pads during a run, that fact lives in the Dock tile of the status grid, not in a fake "Washing Mop Pads" row. With the row gone the Dock section has no commands during the wash, so its explanatory text becomes a footer (see open questions).

h. Area editor, switch dialog, cleaning report

The area editor's map loses its names like every other map; nothing else changes. The "Switch to Area?" alert stays: it now follows the Draw Area... row instead of the segment. Anything else I found while reading their current mockups is listed after the crops.

  • The area editor mockup draws names on its map and hard-codes it (vac.areaEditor passed names: true); decided: it draws none now (the atlas mockup follows).
  • The switch-dialog mockup file is titled as an alert but its sibling in the vacuum-sheet mockup builds the same decision with dv.actionSheet (a bottom-style action sheet) while the code uses .alert (AlertCopy.swift:105-117). The sheet mockup's copy ("Are you sure you want to continue?") also differs from the shipped message.
  • The report row title reads "Main Floor Cleaning Report (4)" under a section header "Cleaning Report": the robot name and the count repeat what the header and the page already say.
  • "Edit Area / Clear Area" on Controls and "Redraw / Clear" in the editor use the same trash glyph with different labels.
  • "Power Settings" holds Mode, Fan and Water, while Passes sits in the Controls list above it; the recommendation puts all four under "Cleaning Settings".

4. Recommendation

Take every recommended option. The three complaints are one problem: the sheet uses a different control grammar for each row. The recommendation gives one tile grammar for status, one pop-up grammar for choices, one switch grammar for on and off, and one screen for choosing rooms and cleaning them. Each part is independent; if you want less, the alternatives above drop in one by one (the 3-up strip and "keep five sections" are the cheapest).

What it touches

  • Map: ValetudoMapView.swift (remove drawNames, keep order badges and the hatch). Pattern patterns/valetudo-map.md: reverse "Room names are always drawn" (owner 2026-10-24) with the 2026-10-28 decision.
  • Status and Locate: VacuumMapPanel.swift (VacuumStatusChip FlowLayout becomes a four-tile grid, Locate leaves the map), VacuumComponents.swift (new tile view; VacuumSettingRow becomes pop-up only, or pop-up rows beside the existing segmented path if the alternative is chosen).
  • Structure: VacuumTabs.swift (VacuumControlsTab absorbs VacuumRoomsTab; target picker and the Selected Rooms row go; area row; VacuumAutoCleanTab toggles), VacuumSheet.swift (tab list, VacuumRuntime.tabs), ModalTabBar.swift (titled form for four or fewer sections at compact width). Pattern patterns/vacuum-sheet.md.
  • Atlas: update vacuum-sheet, vacuum-area-editor and vacuum-switch-dialog mockups in the same PR; hub republish.
  • Tests: journeys that tap the vacuum.target segments (VacuumJourneyTests, VacuumFlowTests, LightSheets journeys) and anything reading vacuum.tabs change; new accessibility tests on iPhone, Duo and iPad for the tile grid at AX5 (one column), the pop-up rows, the switch rows (name, role, value) and the four-item bar; unit tests for the merged tab's selection and area state; a scroll-cost measurement for the longer Clean section.
  • New motion: none. Colour cross-fades on room cards and tiles reuse the state token.
