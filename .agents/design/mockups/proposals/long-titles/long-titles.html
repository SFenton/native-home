Long page titles carousel in the bar

The collapsed title was centred by the system. It now leads the bar beside the menu button or Back, and when it is wider than the room between the bar's items it scrolls like a ticker instead of being cut. Title: "Bedroom 1 Walk-in Wardrobe and Dressing Area".

Cause

  • Large-title pages (iPhone, iPad with the flyout): once scrolled the system draws a centred inline title.
  • Title-in-the-bar pages (iPhone Duo, a compact iPad window; ShellChrome.titleInHeader): the system's large inline title truncates with an ellipsis, and on iPad it also centres once collapsed. On the Duo the system hides the bar title once the page scrolls (baseline measured on iOS 27; unchanged).
  • A leading toolbar item gets almost no width from the bar (a 4-letter title cut to "Ho..."), and forcing it wide pushes every header button into a "…" overflow menu. Measured on iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0.

iPhone and iPad with the flyout: large title below the bar

iPhone Duo and compact iPad: title in the bar

Text sizes and Reduce Motion

  • Accessibility text sizes (AX1 to AX5): the bar title scales with Dynamic Type (.headline / .title2) and still ticks; it never wraps in the bar and never runs under the header buttons (the bar clamps it). The large title below the bar wraps as the system does.
  • Reduce Motion: no ticker (ticker rules). In the bar the title holds still on one line and is cut with an ellipsis (truncatesWhenStill), since a bar cannot grow to wrap. VoiceOver speaks the whole title either way; the large title below the bar shows more of it.
  • VoiceOver: the ticker text is hidden; the bar title is one element with the title as its label and the header trait. At rest on large-title pages the bar has no title (the large title is the heading), so there is never a duplicate.

Options considered

OptionResult
A. A SwiftUI .topBarLeading toolbar itemRejected. The bar gives it almost no width; forcing it wide overflows every header item into "…".
B. A SwiftUI .principal itemRejected. Not clamped to the room between items (an over-wide one overflows under the header buttons), and a flexible one is laid out 0 pt wide on iPad.
C. A UIKit titleView hosting the tickerChosen. A title view that asks for more than it needs is given exactly the room between the leading and trailing items, left-aligned. The scroll observer is UIKit, so a scroll never updates SwiftUI's graph.
