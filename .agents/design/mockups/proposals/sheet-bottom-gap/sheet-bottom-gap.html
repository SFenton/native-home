The gap under every fitted sheet

Every content-fitted sheet sits 34 pt taller than its content. The extra is an empty band between the last control and the home-indicator area. Should the fit stop asking for it?

Cause

hassSheet() fitted (modal-sheet.md, Height) asks the system for a custom detent of content + header + bottom safe area. iOS measures a custom detent up from the bottom safe area and adds that inset itself, so the safe area is counted twice. The sheet is detent + 34 tall, the page area is the detent, and 34 pt of ground sits under the content. This is the "34 pt of empty ground" flagged to the owner earlier and left unchanged; this proposal asks what to do about it.

Recommendation

B. HIG Sheets asks for the smallest detent that shows the content, and HIG Layout keeps content clear of the home indicator through the safe area, not through extra ground. The 34 pt is a side effect of how iOS measures a custom detent, not a design choice anyone made, and the content already carries its own bottom padding (the camera controls row is 136 pt including it). It also helps the Duo: the folded Front Door viewer stops being pinned at the large detent.

What it asks of the app. One change in SheetFit / SheetFitModel: the wanted height stops adding the bottom inset (and the chrome prediction in SheetChromeMemory follows). Every fitted sheet changes, so this is app-wide: all hassSheet() fitted sheets get 34 pt shorter on iPhone and the folded Duo. The fitted-sheet journeys (CameraViewerFitJourneyTests and the others that assert a height) update their expected heights by 34 pt, modal-sheet.md loses its "flagged, unchanged" note, and the atlas mockups of fitted sheets are redrawn. Risks to settle with the probe first: that the page does not re-add its own bottom inset once the detent shrinks, and that isCutShort and maxSheetHeight (page area measure) still agree.

iPad: to be measured before building. Its form and page sheets are sized by presentationSizing, so B may not apply there, or may need a different rule; this proposal does not change iPad until the probe says.
