# Action and navigation tiles

> **What:** the decided convention for tiles that do something, tiles that open something, and tiles that only show state, plus the motion of their action buttons. **Read when:** adding, changing or reviewing any tile with an action, a chevron, or no action at all; building the action strip.

Proposal: [action-navigation-tiles.html](mockups/proposals/action-navigation-tiles/action-navigation-tiles.html) (in the [mockup hub](mockups/README.md)), architecture: [architecture.md](mockups/proposals/action-navigation-tiles/architecture.md). **Owner picked option B with every refinement on 2026-10-26**; the open questions take the recommended defaults below until the owner answers them otherwise.

## Goal

It was not clear which tiles act and which navigate, actions were not visible on tiles, and some navigation tiles should also act (owner, 2026-10-26). Today one tile (the bathroom fan) has in-tile buttons, drawn by a feature-local `FanRailButton`; `BrightnessTile` has its own power button.

## The decision (owner, option B)

- **Shape tells the class.** An action tile reads *text · disc*: no leading glyph, the 44 pt trailing disc carries the state glyph (lock.fill / lock.open, power, garage door), and tapping the body does what the disc shows. A navigation tile reads *glyph · text · chevron*. A read-only tile keeps its glyph and goes flat (no sheen or rim, no press response, no chevron or disc). Rule: a leading glyph means "not an action tile".
- **Placement.** Up to two actions trail on any tile, vertically centred; on a navigation tile they sit left of the chevron's reserve (today's fan rail geometry). Three or more actions and custom controls (a playing remote's transport and volume) go centred below the text. At accessibility sizes trailing discs drop below the text. **The chevron is vertically centred at every tile height.**
- **Discs.** Toggle-styled discs show state; momentary discs show the action (pause while playing). Resting disc on a dark tile: white 16% with the family glyph at 3:1 or better; on: solid white with the family fill as glyph. ≥ 12 pt between discs. Errors differ by shape (jammed `lock.trianglebadge.exclamationmark`; "Didn't Respond" keeps its glyph in red); unavailable dims the disc.
- **Feedback kinds.** *Momentary* (next, previous, locate, dock): press highlight and `TapFeedback.tap`, nothing else. *Optimistic* (play/pause, fan, towel rack, purifier, guest toggles): glyph swaps at once, failure animates back. *Tracked* (lock, garage, PC): animates to the transitional state (arc spinner in the disc, or the moving role) and out to the final glyph and fill.
- **Garage door: one morphing disc.** Closed → Open, open → Close, moving → Stop when the cover supports it (`supported_features & 8`), else Reverse (today's behaviour), Sending/Stopping → arc spinner. Ours are Aladdin Connect, `supported_features` 3 (live check 2026-10-26): they reverse and never show "Partly Open". The tile stays filled whenever the door is not closed.
- **Long text widens, then tickers.** Text is one line; a tile's width comes from its title and its longest possible state, so live updates never re-span the grid. The grid widens the tile track by track (iPhone half → full row; iPad 1 → 2 → 3 tracks; never across the hinge band). Only a tile already spanning its row or side tickers its overflowing line (`TickerLabel`); Reduce Motion and accessibility sizes wrap instead.
- **Availability.** One action becoming unavailable dims in place, never disappears. When a tile's action *set* changes shape (off remote ⇄ playing remote) its height animates with `standard` and discs fade with `quick`: an **owner-approved exception to motion.md §6**, limited to full-row tiles, deferred while the page scrolls, never on reconnect or snapshot reapply; Reduce Motion cross-fades.
- **Remote tiles.** Off: Power beside the chevron. Playing: previous / play-pause / next and a volume slider below, the play-pause disc emphasized.

## Open questions: defaults in force (owner may override)

1. Body tap on a two-action tile does the **primary (leading)** action (none exist today).
2. An off remote shows **Power beside the chevron**.
3. Volume is a **slider** (the `BrightnessTile` horizontal-pan approach, writes on release).
4. Front Door **keeps one-tap unlock** on the tile body (today; HIG: no alert for a common, undoable action).
5. First build: **remotes, bathroom fan, vacuum** (plus every action-1 tile: locks, garage, PCs, towel racks, guest toggles). Air purifier, Security System and beds follow once the strip is proven, each with the owner's go.
6. Action tiles **drop the leading glyph** (two to a row on iPhone).

## Rollout to every page (owner, 2026-10-27)

The owner wants action and navigation tiles on **every page in the app**. The audit of tiles not yet converted is in `build/handoff/tile-convention-audit.md` (35 items, 7 proposed exemptions); exemptions and mockups follow the owner's marks. Answers to the audit's questions:

- **B. Chevrons:** every navigation tile carries the chevron, including tiles that open a sheet (Custom Lights tiles and the sprinkler controller card gain one).
- **C. Confirmed discs:** a disc keeps today's confirmation where one exists (bed off, High AQI, Vacation Mode, Solo Trip, disarm); Front Door stays one tap.
- **D. Light discs:** lights and switches use the `power` glyph, matching PCs and towel racks.
- **A. Tiles that choose one of several** (owner, 2026-10-27, option C of [selection-tiles](mockups/proposals/selection-tiles/selection-tiles.html)): single choice (Studio sources, Solo Trip travelers) uses the **selected face**: the chosen tile fills in its family colour with a white `checkmark` disc, the others stay dark with an empty `circle` disc; VoiceOver reads "selected", never "switch". Multi-select with an order (vacuum rooms) uses the same filled face with a **numbered disc** (the cleaning order) when chosen and `circle` when not. A switch (iOS `Toggle`) stays for independent on/off items only, which already use the toggle-styled power disc.
- **Rollout sub-choices** (owner, 2026-10-27, the recommendations in [tile-rollout-rooms-media](mockups/proposals/tile-rollout-rooms-media/tile-rollout-rooms-media.html) and [tile-rollout-settings-more](mockups/proposals/tile-rollout-settings-more/tile-rollout-settings-more.html)): vents stay without a disc on the room page and read-only in the climate sheets (items 3, 16); Home overview Lights room cards gain a room power disc (12); the Quick Links Security System disc offers **Arm Home** while disarmed and **Disarm** (confirmed) while armed (17); car Doors on Settings gets the lock disc (25); Chores quick links move the open count into the subtitle ("3 open · 2 overdue") and drop the numeral (26). Exempt as other patterns: remote app artwork tiles, camera tiles, status and room chips, alarm mode buttons, the Thermostat Hub pill, status pills and weather readouts, the inventory row's cart button.
- **Studio sources** (owner, 2026-10-27, items 5 and 7): tapping the already-selected source keeps today's behaviour and turns the TV off (its check disc's VoiceOver label is "Turn Off TV", tracked to the off state); with Game active, Game Console and Game both read selected, as today.
- **Handheld Console and Media Room streaming box** (owner, 2026-10-27, items 6 and 8): a toggle-styled `power` disc with **tracked** feedback. Off: the disc runs the tile's own script (`script.demo_media_room_2`, `script.demo_media_room_1`). On: the disc reads "Turn Off" and runs `script.demo_media_room_1`, the remote's Power key, which takes its turn-off branch whenever the tile shows On (owner's option 1; a dedicated `script.demo_example_1` in Home Assistant may replace it later). On tap the disc swaps to the arc spinner and stays in flight while the script runs (the `script.*` entity is `on`) and until the tile's `input_boolean.demo_input_boolean_*_active` reports the new state, then animates to the final on or off glyph and fill; failure animates back with "Didn't Respond".

## Rules this adds

- HIG departures, recorded: discs beside a disclosure chevron (Lists and tables; mitigated by the 12 pt gap, 44 pt targets and today's fan rail); ticker instead of wrapping on tiles (Typography; the motion.md §9 ticker exception gains tiles); layout animation on a live update (motion.md §6 exception above).
- One component: `TileActionButtonStyle` replaces `FanRailButton` and `BrightnessTile.powerButton`; pattern record [action-tiles](../patterns/action-tiles.md). Domain rules live in HassCore providers (`TileActionProviding`), never in views.
- Audit departure, recorded: the accessibility audit reads a disc as a button and wants 4.5:1, while disc glyphs are held to WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 (owner's decision, enforced by `TileActionInkTests`) and are exempted in the journeys' audit.
- Haptics follow [tap-feedback](../patterns/tap-feedback.md): one tap haptic on the tap, no late success haptic.
- Build order, tests and the architecture: [architecture.md](mockups/proposals/action-navigation-tiles/architecture.md). motion.md §6, motion-components.md, entity-tile.md and the atlas mockups (security, rooms, media, fan tile, glass tile) are updated with the build.

## Defaults chosen in the build

Where the decision was silent, the build took these (owner may change any; each is one line in a provider):

- **Lock:** `isOn` means unlocked (filled tile, white disc). A jammed lock offers Lock. A tap shows the lock's own transitional state ("Unlocking…") optimistically, cleared by any live change; this replaces the `isPending` bridge.
- **Garage:** the morphing disc (open, close). Stop never shows: our doors do not support it and `GarageDoorController` has no stop.
- **Media transport:** shown for playing, paused and buffering; idle, on and standby show Power only. Prev and next call `media_player.media_previous_track` / `media_next_track`; play/pause use the remote's own commands. Play/pause is emphasized in every transport state. The volume slider shows only when the volume entity is on and supports `volume_set` (Receiver and Beam excepted, as the remote sheet does).
- **Vacuum:** start is `vacuum.start`; dock is `house.fill`, momentary.
- **Power discs:** towel racks and PCs use the `power` glyph.
- **Guest toggles:** send explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off` (ids `guest.tile.<entity>`).
- **Car (vehicle) tile:** drops its car glyph for the lock disc.
- **Fan:** the lock disc dims in place when unavailable.
- **Width:** iPad item tiles take at least 2 tracks (150 pt tracks). A capped tile keeps its discs trailing until the 96 pt text floor, then the strip drops below.
- **Rollout foundations (lane F, owner decisions left open):** the Studio source check disc reads `sensor.demo_media_room_2` (today's source of truth) rather than an `input_boolean.demo_input_boolean_*_active`, which only Switch and streaming box have; Game, which has no off branch today, keeps relaunching when tapped while chosen (the other three turn the TV off); the bed's turn-off question keeps the mockup's words and a destructive confirmation dialog anchored to the tile ("Turn off Alex's Bed?"), and every other confirmation is an alert with a plain confirming button; Switch and streaming box wait up to 45 s for the boolean before "Didn't Respond"; an alarm that is `arming` or `pending` offers a tappable, confirmed Disarm (pending is the entry delay, when Disarm matters most; changed in review), and `disarming` offers Arm Home in flight; the Stop Watering disc dims while idle (the mockup's recommendation); a selection tile's ring and chosen disc are 28 pt inside the 44 pt target; the `power` disc glyph is an outline in the mockup kit.
- **Rollout L (lane L, build defaults):** the Security System link spans two tracks on a phone because its title, state, disc and chevron do not fit one (the mockup draws it `wide`), so it is the one Quick Links tile wider than the rest and leaves a gap after Rooms in the two-column grid (the grid packs left to right); an unavailable Custom Lights tile has no disc (mockup 11) while an unavailable action light tile dims its disc in place; the Lights room disc targets the room's group light (every room has one) and the overview card shows its new state at once; the redacted build draws no alarm disc; vent tiles stay read-only in all three places.
- **Rollout S (lane S, build defaults):** Front Door Auto-Lock draws the mockup's `lock.fill` / `lock.open` disc (the task's "power disc" gave way to the approved mockup); the Settings switches keep `HouseholdSettingsController` as their one optimistic path instead of a second `OptimisticActions`, since it already owns the 2 s revert, the failed face and the spoken refusal; High AQI keeps its alert as the disc's confirmation, with ids `<tile-id>.confirm` / `.cancel` replacing `household.tile.confirm`; Guest Controls take the Home page's face and ids; car Doors takes the Security page's lock rules on the Settings blue (owner pick 25 B).
- **Rollout M (lane M, build defaults):** Chores links show "N open · M overdue" / "Nothing open" (Groceries "N open"), the web's longer wording stays in `ChoresConfig` for parity; Vacation Mode and Solo Trip ask their existing question from the tile (their alerts moved off the screens) and keep the dates sheet and the editor as the gate for turning on; Solo Trip travelers borrow the Vents blue family because a selection fills in a *family* colour and Solo Trip's own blue is not one; the sprinkler card does not pass `isUnavailable` so an offline controller still opens its sheet; the Selected Rooms order chips sit under the tile (the Swift tile has no under slot) and are hidden from VoiceOver because the tile speaks the order; the hint "Asks for the vacation dates" and its siblings are replaced by the tile's own verb hint.
