# Duo widths: one section column in portrait, buttons fill their section

> **What:** how many section columns a page shows by pose, and how a short row of tiles fills its section (lone tiles, a row of 3 in 4 tracks, the final unpaired section). **Read when:** you touch `UKGridSolver`, `DynamicGridMetrics`, `UKDynamicGridView`, `UKFlexGridView`, `UKRoomsGrid`, a section grid (340 pt) or any tile grid, or you add a grid that must not fill.

## The request

Owner, 2026-11-03 (verbatim in `build/briefs/T-duo-widths-plan.md`): Duo portrait, folded and unfolded, "restricted to one column width max"; Duo landscape "up to four elements width max, two columns (so two side by side sections get two width each: a final section with no section to its right may take up to four width)"; "Grids should still be dynamic, filling available space as they can. Buttons should fill space they can in their sections"; in the Admin screenshot each lone tile should span the whole row, "a four-width button"; "Most of the app already looks good but some buttons fall into this trap (wake lights in bedroom 1)".

**Goal.** No button in a section leaves the rest of its row empty; a portrait Duo never shows two sections side by side at any text size; landscape keeps two section columns of two tile widths each, and a section with nothing beside it takes all four.

## Decisions

Decision: 2026-11-04, judged fill styles A (integer spans: spare tracks handed out round-robin from the last item, the web's `packDynamicGridSpans(..., 'all')`; 3 tiles in 4 tracks become 1, 1, 2), B (equal shares: a short row's width divided equally among its items, never below the width each already has; 3 in 4 become three equal tiles) and C (fill only a section whose tiles make one row); picked B as the default the Coder builds, with A built behind `FENTON_GRID_FILL=spans` for comparison, because B gives every button in a row the same size (HIG Buttons, "Same-size buttons read as a set; mixed sizes nearby look inconsistent") and reads as the owner's "fill the space they can", while A makes one arbitrary tile (the last) twice the others: Guests' Media Room would look more important than Guest Room. ## Rules this adds

1. **Short rows fill (rule 3).** In every tile grid and every section grid, a row whose items leave tracks unused (a lone tile, a short last row, a row cut short because the next item did not fit) is filled across its width (one side's width while a band crosses the grid), and no item gets less than its placed span. **A grid of one row** shares that width equally (max-min fair share: items whose own width exceeds the equal share keep it; the rest share what is left), so 3 in 4 tracks are three equal thirds. **A grid of more than one row** fills in whole tracks so every edge stays on the track lines of the other rows: starting from each item's span, the spare tracks go one at a time to the narrowest item, ties to the later item in reading order (4 + 3 is 4 + 1, 1, 2; Quick Links' 2 in 3 is 1, 2; Vent 1 + Purifier 2 in 4 is 2, 2). Per side while a band crosses the grid. Heights are measured at the filled widths. Headings already span and are untouched. An item that ends up spanning its whole row or side is `fullRow` (the ticker and tile-animation gate). Supersedes the 2026-10-30 "short last row starts at the leading edge" and "never stretch a lone item" clauses of [dynamic-grid](../patterns/dynamic-grid.md) for every grid not listed in rule 4.
2. **Grids stay dynamic.** The track count is still the most that fit at the scaled 150 pt minimum, at most four; filling only distributes spare width inside a row, never changes the count, never moves an item to another row and never shrinks one.
3. **A band is a wall.** A fill never crosses a hinge band: partly folded, each side of a row fills its side only (`spanCap` semantics). Lying flat there is no band, so a lone tile spans the full row: the owner's "four-width button".
4. **Grids that never fill** (`fill: .none`, short rows stay leading at the track width):
   - Small controls: any grid whose minimum is under `DynamicGridMetrics.controlMaxWidth` (100 pt): colour swatches, weekday chips, alarm mode buttons, the vacuum status tile grid. Automatic in the solver.
   - Aspect-ratio media, whose height follows width so a filled tile would grow tall: Cameras (`UKCameraTileView` grid), Quick App Launch (`UKRoomAppLaunchGrid`), remote app tiles (`UKMediaRemoteTabs`), Recipes (not a solver consumer; untouched). HIG Collections: "Prefer the familiar standard horizontal row or grid".
   - Choice chips (`UKChoiceGrid`: Wake Light ramp and hold, Presence option rows `UKFlexGridView(300, 2)`): they are one choice among equal options, so they keep one size (HIG Segmented controls, "In general, keep segment size consistent"); a full-width lone "30 Minutes" would read as a primary action (HIG Buttons, "Prefer full-width buttons for primary actions").
   - Dials (Humidifier schedule, 260 / 2): a pair that never makes a short row; marked `.none` for intent.
   - The performance gallery's `UKDynamicGridLayout` workload (scroll-cost baseline).
5. **Section columns (rule 1).** A section grid (the room grid, Media, Sprinklers' controllers, the Vacation two-up grids: every 340 pt section minimum) scales its minimum with `.body` up only: `max(340, scaled 340)`. Two section columns therefore need 704 pt of content (two 340 pt columns and the 24 pt gap) at every text size. Unfolded portrait Duo (637 pt): one column always. Unfolded landscape lying flat (836 pt): two. Folded (383 and 563 pt): one. iPhone: one. iPad portrait (802 pt): two at default as today, one at accessibility sizes as today. Section grids never exceed two columns on the Duo by width alone (three need 1068 pt); iPad landscape Media keeps its three.
6. **Final unpaired section (rule 2).** By rule 1 applied to the section grid: flat, a lone last section spans both section columns and its tiles take up to four tracks; partly folded it keeps its side (section-sides rule 6), never spanning the band.
7. **The Climate case.** A one-track Vent beside a two-track Air Purifier in a four-track row becomes two equal halves (the purifier keeps at least its two tracks; the Vent takes the rest), matching the Bedroom 1 row today. Where the purifier claims the whole row (`isFullWidth`), the Vent sits on its own row and fills it. Same-size buttons in a row (HIG Buttons).
8. **Motion.** A fill change (rotation, a pose change, a tile appearing in a section: an open contact sensor, a guest toggle) moves frames through `UKReflow` on `MotionTokens.standard`; Reduce Motion cross-fades in `quick`. Nothing re-fades or reorders (no reload on reopen). HIG Collections: "make dynamic layout changes sensible and easy to track".
9. **Accessibility.** Filling only widens; the 44 pt targets, reading order (row by row, leading to trailing) and AX5 behaviour (one track, so every row is already full) are unchanged. Wider tiles mean fewer clipped titles: the Admin titles that clip at one track fit at full width.

## HIG

- Buttons: "Same-size buttons read as a set; mixed sizes nearby look inconsistent." (fill style B; the Climate case.) "Prefer full-width buttons for primary actions." (why choice chips do not fill.)
- Segmented controls: "In general, keep segment size consistent." (choice chips.)
- Layout: "Align related elements; indentation communicates subordination. Group related information/functions using negative space, containers, or separators." (one section column in a tall window; whole tracks in a grid of more than one row, judged on the after pack.)
- Designing for iPhone Duo: "Expand the existing layout with space; use margins/safe-area insets and avoid fixed widths or display-specific dependencies"; "Prefer a layout container that adapts automatically, and even grid column counts. Avoid extreme changes: move only what's necessary". (Every family, geometric rules; the even count is untouched.)
- Collections: "make dynamic layout changes sensible and easy to track" (rule 8).
- Web parity: the web fills rows by default (`DynamicGrid` `fillRows = true`, `lastRow 'fill'`, `ha-sfenton-react-dash-insensitive-20260917@11cdab2:src/components/core/DynamicGrid.tsx:220,235`) with integer spans handed out from the last item (style A). Departures: a grid of one row shares equally, and a grid of more than one row gives spare tracks to the narrowest item first (a Vent beside a Purifier is 2 : 2, not the web's 1 : 3), both for the HIG Buttons reason above; a 4 + 3 row matches the web (1, 1, 2).
