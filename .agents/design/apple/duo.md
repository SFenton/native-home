# iPhone Duo

> **What:** foldable iPhone adaptation. **Read when:** changing layout that a fold or hinge can cross.

- Rotates freely folded and unfolded (`FentonOrientationSupport` allows all orientations once `HingePresence` sees a hinge or vertical bar). Folded and unfolded portrait are single page; unfolded landscape may split ([split-view](split-view.md)).
- Carry over FestivalNativeApps' Duo rules, with the owner's 2026-10-24 posture rule (in flight, mockups only; the app still splits on the hinge in every pose): on the inner display the on-demand split divider sits **on the hinge while the Duo is slightly closed** (book pose) and at the **midpoint of the free space** (window minus the trailing rail) **while it is fully open** ([split-view](split-view.md)); the outer display behaves as an iPhone.
- Run the `apple-hig` skill's `/duo` workflow stage by stage before Duo work.
- **The hinge in layout** (plan Phase 4, 2026-10-15). `EnvironmentValues.windowHinge` (HassDesign; the shell reads it once, `readsWindowHinge()`) is the one source. Every rule below is geometric (a hinge rect), never a device-name check, and is unit tested by feeding a rect measured on the simulator (hinge band 455.3 to 495.7 pt in a 951 pt window, content ending at the trailing bar's 868 pt).
  - **Even columns.** A `DynamicGridLayout` rounds its column count down to an even number (at least two) while a hinge is set, so no track pair straddles the fold ([dynamic-grid](../../patterns/dynamic-grid.md)).
  - **Tab bar when unfolded (owner, 2026-10-26).** The root tab bar stays (`ShellNavigationStyle.resolve(isRegularWidth:hasHinge:)`: a window with a hinge keeps `.tabBar`); on the unfolded landscape simulator the system draws it in the trailing vertical bar, icons only, with no hamburger. iPad (no hinge) keeps the flyout.
  - **Chip gap.** A `ChipRail` (Home, Security, room pages) scrolls as one row across the whole page and over the hinge (owner, 2026-10-26: "a horizontal rail of scrollable status chips across the whole page", replacing the 24 pt stop). Only an accessibility-size wrapped rail still stops 24 pt short of the hinge (`ChipRailMetrics.hingeGap`). The system's trailing vertical bar also covers about 83 pt and sits in the safe area.
  - **Two-column Home.** In unfolded landscape Home splits on the hinge (`HomeColumns`, built on `SplitPolicy.geometry`): the chip rail spans the full page above both columns (crossing the hinge), then weather leads and cameras and guests trail, the hinge band stays empty below it (mockup n1-duo-unfolded). Cameras take the 2 x 2 layout in the trailing half. iPad landscape stays one column (n1-ipad-home), and unfolded portrait is one column.
  - **Quick Links and Profile** are header buttons, not a floating circle (owner, 2026-10-16; reshaped 2026-10-18, option A, [direction-g](../direction-g.md) Navigation). On the unfolded Duo the system moves bar items into its trailing vertical bar; the folded Duo's vertical rail does too. Measured on the folded Duo simulator (iOS 27.1, 2026-10-18): Quick Links (a plain symbol item) goes into the rail and is found there as a button, while the Profile monogram, a custom view, **stays in the horizontal top bar** (a custom view cannot go vertical; FestivalNativeApps avoids that by showing the symbol `person.crop.circle.fill` in the vertical bar, S+H keeps the monogram on the owner's mockup, "on Duo the avatar stays in the horizontal bar"). The disc is still 44 pt. The header adds no `ToolbarSpacer(.fixed)` in a vertical bar (`shellInVerticalBar`, set from `toolbarVerticalEdge`; HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "system spacing adapts, so don't add fixed spacing").
  - Verified on the `iPhone Duo (HASS)` simulator unfolded in landscape (2026-10-16): Home splits (the chips then stopped short of the hinge; since 2026-10-26 the rail spans the page), the weather card on compact paging (its hourly row pages by the compact count in the half-width column, nine hours squeezed 400 pt), cameras 2 x 2 in the trailing half and the Quick Links circle bottom trailing (`AdaptiveLayoutJourneyTests.testHomeSplitsOnTheHingeOnlyWhenUnfoldedInLandscape`). Unfolded portrait and the partly folded pose were not run.
  - **A rule for anything that reads the hinge:** measure the full-width frame *outside* any padding that depends on the hinge. `ChipRail` first measured inside its own trailing padding, so the inset shrank the frame it was computed from, flipped every layout pass and pinned the main thread at 100% CPU on the unfolded Duo (journeys timed out with "Unable to perform work on main run loop"). The unit tests cannot see that; only the unfolded simulator did.
- **Page title on the vertical bar (owner, 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30; [header-edge-fade](../../patterns/header-edge-fade.md) R7 (6)).** Owner, 2026-10-30, verbatim: "Unfolded portrait should just use the normal iPhone style title. Unfolded landscape/partial landscape should use 24pt". Wherever the system draws the bars on the trailing vertical edge (`shellInVerticalBar`, from `toolbarVerticalEdge`: folded in both rotations, unfolded landscape, and partial landscape where it reports the edge, see R7) the title is a fixed 24 pt semibold inline title (scaled with Dynamic Type, capped at 30 pt) in a bar that never minimises (`BarTitleTreatment.fixedMedium`), so it is there at rest, while a page scrolls, after a tab switch and after push and Back. Before 2026-10-29 the system's small title disappeared when the bar minimised on scrolling. Unfolded portrait has horizontal bars and keeps the iPhone's system title (large, collapsing; leading aligned beside the hamburger). In unfolded landscape a split's trailing pane has no title of its own (the placeholder "Select an item to edit it" on Chores); the leading pane's title is the page's. HIG, Designing for iPhone Duo: the vertical controls "stay on the same side"; "navigation-focused views preserve the tab bar". The treatment follows the window, so a rotation between landscape and portrait changes the title style in place, and the page keeps its state (the view tree is the same in both).
- Window changes (fold, unfold, rotate) move an open detail between the trailing pane and a push, one run-loop turn after the window update, unanimated.

## Duo rail (owner, 2026-10-20)

No hamburger on the Duo (folded or vertical rail). In the vertical rail Quick Links and Profile sit in one shared glass group (`ToolbarItemGroup`, Profile as the person symbol, no fixed spacer (owner, 2026-10-20; a monogram custom view stays in the horizontal bar, so the rail group shows the symbol)), replacing the two circles of 2026-10-18. See [direction-g](../direction-g.md).
