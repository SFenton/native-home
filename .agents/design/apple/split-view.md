# Screen classification and split view

> **What:** device orientation rules, when splits are allowed, and how each screen is presented. **Read when:** adding a screen or changing layout on iPad or iPhone Duo.

## Orientation and split rules (owner, 2026-10-10)

| Device / pose | Orientation | Split |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone | Portrait only (Info.plist `~iphone`) | Never |
| iPhone Duo folded (outer display) | Any (runtime, once a hinge is seen) | Never: single page |
| iPhone Duo unfolded portrait | Any | Never: single page |
| iPhone Duo unfolded landscape | Any | Allowed; divider on the hinge |
| iPad portrait | Any | Never: single page |
| iPad landscape | Any | Allowed; divider at the midpoint |

Implementation: `HassUI/App/Layout/SplitPolicy.swift` (regular width, wider than tall, each half ≥ 360 pt) and `OrientationPolicy.swift` + `DeviceSignals.swift` (ported from FestivalNativeApps). Never branch on a device name.

## Hinge posture rule (owner, 2026-10-24; binding 2026-11-01; shipped)

| Duo inner display | Divider |
|---|---|
| Slightly closed (book pose) | On the hinge: leading pane ends at the band, trailing pane starts after it (455.3 / 40.4 / 372.3 pt in 868 pt of content) |
| Fully open (flat) | At the midpoint of the free space, the window minus the trailing rail, with a 1 pt band (433 / 1 / 434 pt) |

iPad landscape always uses the midpoint. This replaces "on the hinge in every pose" (owner, 2026-10-10). Lying flat, **nothing avoids the hinge**: a row of like elements has no middle gap (the Doors tiles on Security), a single object centres in the free space, two-pane screens, on-demand splits and Home's columns divide at the free space's midpoint with a 1 pt band, and a sheet opens in the leading half and ends where the page divides (`DuoSheetPolicy.leadingHalfWidth(fold:freeWidth:)`). When the layout changes position (partly folded to flat and back, fold, unfold, rotation) content animates its reflow with `MotionTokens.standard` (`MotionTokens.quick`, 0.2 s, under Reduce Motion): the shell's reader changes `windowFold` inside `withAnimation`.

**Status:** shipped in the SwiftUI app (2026-11-01, since deleted). The shared model is `WindowFold` (`HassDesign/WindowFold.swift`): `readsWindowHinge()` publishes `EnvironmentValues.windowFold` (the hinge in either pose, `isFolding` true while partly folded), `windowHinge` (its `band`, set only while partly folded, which is all avoidance reads) and `windowFreeWidth` (window less the trailing vertical bar). Presence consumers (shell style, sheet fill, placement and half width, zoom) read `windowFold.hinge`; avoidance consumers (grid, chip rail, `avoidsHinge`, `clearsHinge`, `HingedPanes`, `OnDemandSplitStack`) read `windowHinge`. Drawn in the mockups ([mockups](../mockups/README.md): the Hinge control, and the Reflow and hinge section).

**UIKit port: shipped (2026-11-01).** `UKDeviceSignals` publishes the shared `WindowFold` (`UKShellEnvironment.windowFold`, the hinge in either pose, for presence: shell style, sheets) and `windowHinge` as the *band*, set only while partly folded. Lying flat the band is nil, so `SplitPolicy.geometry` / `columnWidths` divide their container at its midpoint with the 1 pt band (the container is the page's free space, the window less the trailing rail), grids have no middle gap and single objects centre. The posture change reflows with `MotionTokens.standard` (`UKReflow`: the fold is written once in `UKDeviceSignals.refresh`, laid out inside the spring one run-loop turn later; Reduce Motion cross-fades a snapshot in 0.2 s). Details: [uikit-port](../../platforms/apple/uikit-port.md).

## Column navigation (UIKit port, owner 2026-11-01)

One pattern for page, sub-page and split navigation: `UKColumnsViewController` (`App/Layout`). It supersedes `UKOnDemandSplit` (removed) and the per-page `UKHingedPanes` splits for page-to-page navigation; `UKHingedPanes` stays for true two-pane pages that are not navigation (Vacuums, the Food hub, Recipes).

- **Miller columns, two visible.** Columns are `[root] + entries`; the window shows the last two. Nothing open: root plus the shared empty state (`UKPanePlaceholderViewController`, "Select a setting to see more options"). Tapping a row opens that page on the right. Drilling from the right slides that page left and the new page comes in on the right; Back reverses it (header button, or a leading-edge pan that scrubs a `UIViewPropertyAnimator`). Reduce Motion cross-fades in place: the pages that stay do not slide (owner, 2026-11-02), and the edge pan still works.
- **One rule.** A page calls `show(_:sender:)`; the stack is cut back to the sender's column, then the page is appended (left column replaces the right, right column drills). Each entry has one host moved by frame; nothing is reparented or reloaded.
- **Geometry.** `SplitPolicy.columnWidths`: on the hinge band while partly folded, at the free-space midpoint with a 1 pt band when flat or on iPad. Pose changes reflow through `UKReflow`.
- **Narrow windows** (iPhone, portrait, folded Duo): `isWide` is false, the same routes are the normal push stack, `show` pushes, with the same motion as the columns (`UKStackMotion`: both pages a full width on `standard` over one still wallpaper, the swipe following the finger; owner, 2026-11-02, [single-page-motion](../single-page-motion.md)); pages open when the window stops splitting are handed to the stack. Route restore is unchanged.
- **Navigator stays the truth for routes.** `UKSectionNavigationController` flips `splitting` (the stack motion stands down while it is on and the root page is a `UKColumnsHosting`, Settings): the stack stays at the root and the root page's columns show `navigator.routes`. Pages with no route (Vacation Mode, a form, an item editor) are local entries.
- **Bars (owner, 2026-11-02; supersedes the per-column bars of 2026-11-01, which the physical Duo rejected).** There is one navigation bar: the section's own, full width, as on every top-level page. It carries the page header and the header edge fade across both columns. The container hosts no navigation controllers and draws no header: a column is a plain content host (a frame-moved `UIView`) in the root page's body, below the header, and its page's scroll content starts under the bar and fades at the same edge as the left column's.
- **Rail groups and overflow (owner, 2026-11-02).** The owner page's navigation item has two separate groups: the column group is the bar's *leading* group (after the flyout's menu button where there is one) and the shell's (Quick Links, Profile, the reconnecting arc) is the trailing group. The column group is the open page's own bar items (Performance's Refresh), then Close for the first page or Back from depth 2; absent when nothing is open. On the Duo's vertical bar the leading group is at the top, so Back/Close sits above Quick Links and Profile, and when the rail runs short (Live Activities take its top) the trailing shell items fold into "More", Profile first, never Back/Close: seen on the simulator with `FENTON_DEBUG_RAIL_EXTRA=8` (eight extra shell items). Elsewhere the groups sit in the bar, Back/Close leading. The group is built once around one Back/Close item (`UKColumnBarHosting.setColumnBar(_:)`): it appears, goes and changes between Back and Close by hiding and restyling that item inside `UKMotion.animate(.standard, reduced: .quick)`, so the bar animates it and the shell group slides to make room (swapping groups did not animate; recorded on the Duo simulator with `UK_RECORD=1 tools/uikit_shot.sh`, drive with `FENTON_DEBUG_COLUMN_STEPS="12:back,15:close"`). The container sets it, found on the nearest ancestor page, so every split built on the container gets it. APIs checked: `pinnedTrailingGroup` (cannot overflow, but sits past the trailing items, below the shell in the rail) and the default trailing groups (Close folded with the shell).
- **Sub-page titles are content.** `UKColumnTitleHeader`: a large, left-aligned heading in the page's own scroll content (a subview above the content origin, the top content inset making room) that scrolls away with it. `UKPageViewController` attaches it by itself while the page is a child of the columns and sets no bar title; pushed on the stack (narrow window) the page keeps its normal bar title. The Inventory editor pane attaches the same component to its list.
- **Debug.** `FENTON_DEBUG_COLUMN_SCROLL=<points>` scrolls the right column's page once, seven seconds in.
- **Adopters.** Settings (routes), Chores (routes) and the Food hub (routes), Inventory (item editor, `setDetail`; title as content, Close in the column group) and the todo page form (below). Home, Security and Climate are not column navigation; partly folded, their sections take sides through fold columns ([section-sides](../section-sides.md)).
- **Routes, not local entries (owner, 2026-11-01).** A Chores quick-link page is `ShellRoute.chores(pageId)` and a Food space is `ShellRoute.foodSpace(rawValue)`, so they are navigator-owned like Settings' topics: Quick Links and a cold launch (`RouteRestore`) restore them, the narrow push and the wide column are the same route, and a rotation moves the page between the two with no state of its own. The host is the top page of the stack when it is a `UKColumnsHosting` with `hostsColumnRoutes`: the Chores root, Settings' root, and the Food hub, which is a page on Home's stack and not a section root, so `UKSectionNavigationController` no longer assumes the root: `ColumnRouting.hostedCount` says how many trailing routes the top page shows (every route for a root host; the Food spaces after the hub's own route), and the stack is reconciled to the rest. A route restored after a hub opened by a launch probe has no hub route before it and stays a push. Opening another page replaces the right column (the left column replaces the right) and trims the navigator to the host.
- **Hosts.** Chores: the todo list (with the quick links as its first row) is column one and the empty column says "Select a row to edit it" until a quick link or a form opens. Food hub: one column wide (recipes, then the spaces; the old two-pane hub form remains only without a shell, in the gallery), "Select a food space to see its items" until a space opens. Recipes still pushes full width (a two-pane page of its own).
- **Owner decisions on the columns (2026-11-02).** (1) The Food hub is one column in landscape, with "Select a food space to see its items" in the second until a space is picked. Asked whether to keep it as built, the owner answered: "Yes". (2) One Add button per window. Asked about the Chores list with a quick-link page open beside it (each column drew its own "+ Add Task"), the owner answered: "Add task should always be overlaid in bottom right unless we're editing a chore? Maybe same for food?" Built: where the window shows columns, the Chores root draws the one floating Add button over the whole content area, at its bottom-right (trailing edge of the right column, same style and inset as the single-page button); it adds to the page being looked at (the open quick-link page with that page's list and assignee defaults, labelled "Add Groceries" for Groceries, else the Chores list's) and is hidden while a chore form is open as a column; the quick-link page keeps its bar only as a hidden spacer that clears its last row. Narrow windows keep each page's own button. Food: nothing doubles (the hub floats nothing; a Recipes or Inventory space draws its one bar, which Inventory already pins over its editor column), so Food is unchanged. See [floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md).
- **Pages that host detail panes sit in a column as sheets.** A todo page or Inventory page that is itself a column has no room for a pane of its own (the Inventory page decides from its content width, so its editor is a sheet; a pane-context todo page builds no columns of its own). The Chores root, which is the whole window's list, opens its form as the next column.
- **Forms are column pages.** The todo form is a plain `UKTodoFormController` in the columns (no navigation controller, no bar of its own): its title is content (`UKColumnTitleHeader`), its Save rides in the column group, and Close/Back is the cancel. `UKColumnPopGuarding` lets a page hold itself open: Close/Back (and the edge pan) wait while a save is in flight (the Inventory editor, the todo form: the bar item is disabled, the edge pan does not start) and ask "Discard changes?" before dropping edits (the todo form). The page calls `pageStateChanged()` when its answer changes. A column form is `pushesWhenNarrow = false` (the page re-presents it as a sheet) and `onUserClose` tells the page to drop its draft.
- **Narrow to wide restores local pages.** Pages pushed with no route while the window was narrow (Vacation Mode under Vacation) are taken off the stack when the window widens and appended to the host's columns after its tagged routes (`UKColumnsViewController.adopt`), in order, unanimated; pages draw no wallpaper of their own (the window's one backdrop is behind the stack and the columns), so moving between the two changes nothing there.

## Two-pane screens on the hinge (decided 2026-10-24)

Food hub, Recipes, Inventory, Vacuums, the Vacuum sheet and the todo pages take their pane widths from `SplitPolicy.columnWidths` through `HingedPanes`: the leading pane ends at the hinge and the trailing pane starts after it on the unfolded Duo, equal halves with a gutter on an iPad. Chore and grocery lists adopt the trailing form pane (see [hinge-avoidance](../../patterns/hinge-avoidance.md)). The classification table below is unchanged.

## On-demand split

From FestivalNativeApps `split-view.md`: a page starts full width; selecting an item splits it 50/50 at the content midpoint (Duo: the hinge) with the detail in the trailing half; Close or Back closes the trailing pane first; fold, unfold or rotation that disallows the split moves the open item onto the push stack, one run-loop turn after the window update, unanimated. No drawn divider (1 pt hairline only under Increase Contrast).

## Implementation

- `HassUI/App/Layout/OnDemandSplit.swift` — `OnDemandSplitStack(path:openItem:alwaysSplit:root:detail:placeholder:)` replaces a section's `NavigationStack`. It owns the leading stack and, where `SplitPolicy.geometry(container:isRegularWidth:hinge:)` returns pane widths, a trailing `NavigationStack` with **Close** (`split.close`, Escape) and container `split.trailing`. Without a split the same `openItem` is pushed on the leading stack (`navigationDestination(item:)`), so fold/rotation keeps the item; turning the split on or off applies one run-loop turn later, unanimated. Opening and closing spring (Reduce Motion: crossfade).
- Measure the space offered, not the panes: the container frame comes from a `frame(minWidth: 0, maxWidth: .infinity, minHeight: 0, maxHeight: .infinity)` around the `HStack`. Measuring the `HStack` kept the landscape width after rotating to portrait (its fixed pane widths), so the split never turned off and overflowed the window; a flexible frame without `minWidth: 0` still grows to its child.
- Geometry (owner, 2026-11-01): partly folded, the band is the Duo hinge when it crosses the container and the leading pane ends at it, the trailing pane starts after it; lying flat, the band is 1 pt at the container's midpoint. The container is the page area, which excludes the trailing vertical bar, so its midpoint is the free space's midpoint. Each pane ≥ 360 pt. `readsWindowHinge()` reports the active division as the fold (`isFolding`), else the inactive one the system still reports while the inner display lies flat (`.includeInactive`), as FestivalNativeApps' `DeviceLayout.splitHinge` does; only the active one becomes the `windowHinge` band the splits use, so a flat Duo splits at the midpoint exactly as an iPad does (the earlier "on the hinge in every inner pose", owner 2026-10-10, is superseded). It reads in `onGeometryChange`; reading on appear and on size changes missed the regions, which arrive after the first layout. Measured on the iPhone Duo simulator, landscape (951 × 669 pt): partly folded, hinge band 455.3–495.7 pt, leading pane ends at the band, trailing pane starts at 495.7 pt; flat, 434 / 1 / 434 pt. No drawn line except a hairline under Increase Contrast at a midpoint.
- Rows set `openItem`, mark the open row selected (`.isSelected`, accent row background).
- First adopter was Settings (`alwaysSplit`, placeholder `settings.placeholder`) until direction G moved Settings into the Account sheet (2026-10-14) and then into its own tab (2026-10-16), where topics push on the tab's stack. `OnDemandSplitStack` has no production adopter now; it stays, with its hosted tests, for the next list/detail page (chore lists, rooms).

## Screen classification

| Kind | Screens |
|---|---|
| Always full width, push | Home, Security, Climate, Chores, room pages, Food & Recipes. Partly folded Duo: the sections of Home, Security, Climate, rooms, Media, Custom Lights and Sprinklers sit wholly on one side of the band (fold columns, [section-sides](../section-sides.md), owner 2026-11-03); this is layout, not a split |
| Split on demand (where allowed above) | Chores lists → item detail; Food hub → Pantry, Fridge, Freezer, Spice Rack, Cabinet; Recipes → recipe |
| Leading pane, detail on the right (where allowed) | Settings: list stays left, topic opens right, placeholder when nothing is selected |
| Sheet | Status chip sheets (lights/rooms, security system, climate, occupancy, contacts), light and climate controls, Daily Report, to-do edit |
| Sheet, content-fitted | Camera viewer (dark; video beside controls in iPad landscape) |
| Full-screen modal | Home Assistant backend settings (web view), sign-in |
