# Chart plot grows with the text size

Decision: at the larger accessibility text sizes the axis labels of `sh.chart@1` overlap, because the plot keeps its stated `height` while the labels grow (Caption 1 is 12/16 at the default size and 43/51 at AX5; three labels need 153 pt and the plot is 76 pt). Three fixes were weighed on the stills in `build/evidence/screens/native-wgchart-ec89fcdd/`: grow the plot, thin the labels to top and baseline, and cap the label size. Growing is picked: the plot is `max(height, levels x the axis label's line height)`, so `height` is the plot's height at the text sizes where the labels fit and its minimum above them. Thinning lost because two labels still need 102 pt in 76 pt from AX4 up, and it shows less than the standard sizes do (HIG Typography, Supporting Dynamic Type: "aim to show as much useful text at the largest accessibility size as at the largest standard size"). Capping lost on the same section: "Make sure your layout adapts to all font sizes". Growing follows HIG Charting data, Best practices: "always keeping details, labels and annotations easy to read". The standard sizes are unchanged (three labels need 69 pt at xxxL).
Replaces: none.
Rule home: `.agents/patterns/server-ui-chart-gauge.md` (arrives with `t3/ni-wgchart`); the `wg-grid` lane writes the rule there and in the Studio contract's `height`.
Gate: `app_wide=no`.
