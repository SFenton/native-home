# Settings rows: one group per module-list section, title3 headers, the page margin

Decision: owner-requested parity (pl-setlist), judged from `build/evidence/parity-loop/pr143-after/*-settings.png` (current against the old app on iPhone, folded Duo and iPad) and `build/research/pl-setlist.md`. Four picks. (1) Grouping: a row in a module list never draws its own fill, corners or hairline; the list draws one 26 pt group, and settings rows carry the inset-grouped metrics (15 pt leading and 17.5 pt trailing content inset, hairline inset 15 pt or 61 pt under a glyph row and 16 pt short of the trailing edge, 15 or 10.5 pt row padding around a 44 pt minimum). The alternative, keeping a card per link, lost: it is the defect the owner named and it splits App Icon from the Blur group. Applying the grouped metrics to every module list lost: it would move the vacuum, recipe, climate and checklist rows nobody asked to change. (2) Section header on Settings: three were weighed, the old 15 pt semibold secondary header, the same header as a new authored section property, and the current title3 bold page section header. Title3 bold is kept. The old header lost because the publication has one section node whose header every page shares, so a Settings-only header needs either a page special case in the binary or a new schema property, and because it is the one section-header font already on record; it also keeps Settings in step with the pages it pushes. Gaps come from the publication's spacing tokens: `xl` (24 pt) between sections and `s` (8 pt) inside one, against the old 28.5, 10 and 8 pt; native keeps no Settings gaps. (3) Regular-width margin: the 20 pt inset-grouped margin is not restored. The list keeps the publication's 16 pt page padding on iPad and the unfolded Duo so it lines up with the large title and the header buttons; a 20 pt margin on one page lost on alignment and on being a native override of authored padding. (4) App Icon row: the row shows a 30 pt preview of the installed icon in the glyph column and the icon's name as the subtitle, supplied by a preferences control the publication places in the Appearance group; a native provider on the link lost on the owner's rule that the app stays generic. Full metrics and checks: `build/design/pl-setlist/spec.md`.
Replaces: in `.agents/patterns/settings-section.md`, the "Measured metrics" line's 20 pt regular-width margin and its 28.5, 10 and 8 pt section gaps, and the `InsetGroupedStack` description of the Settings root; the Settings mockup's 15 pt semibold `SettingsSectionHeader` and 704 pt readable width.
Rule home: `.agents/patterns/module-list.md` (a row never draws its own card; a hairline takes its insets from the row above) and `.agents/patterns/settings-section.md` (the settings row kinds and their metrics; the Settings root's header, gaps and margin).
Gate: `matches_request=yes`.
