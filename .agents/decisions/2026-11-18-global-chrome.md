# Global chrome: a page keeps Quick Links, Profile and the tab navigator however it is reached

Decision: a page, never a sheet or a modal, carries the chrome marked global (today the tab navigator, Quick Links and Profile), however it is reached, including from a tile inside a sheet. A `push` to a page from inside a sheet closes every level of the sheet in one dismissal, then pushes on the section stack of the tab that is showing, so the landed page draws the global chrome; a push to a level of the sheet stays in the sheet; a page already on the stack is popped to; the page under the sheet only closes; an adaptive column is removed and the page opens by the columns' rule in one motion. The owner's picks 1 to 8 (2026-11-18, `build/decisions/parity-loop.md`) are read as: 1 A and 2 A, the sheet closes first and Back leads to the page under it with the sheet closed; 3 iPad shows the system tab bar, at the top, with no sidebar; 4 A with a per-page hide; 5 no "Opens" setting, the Studio tab an item lives in is the rule; 6 A with the system Back chevron and no text; 7 wide windows default to Automatic and are configurable per page; 8 the 121 pages in sheets are classified by the old app. Options A, B and C judged for the motion: A sheet first then push (picked, the old app's `quick-links-to-page` flow); B swap under the sheet (no push seen); C both at once (two motions at once). Options A and B judged for Back: A the page under the sheet (picked); B reopen the sheet (two motions for one Back; the swipe cannot scrub the second). Also judged and rejected: a "Global" switch, a per-link choice, an "Opens" setting and always full width. This change lands the native sheet rule only (`gc-runtime-sheet-origin`); the rule applies to a document that declares `levels` (`sh.sheet-levels@1`), so a lowered document keeps every push inside the sheet as a build before the capability reads it.
Replaces: the iPad flyout rule of `.agents/design/apple/ipados.md` (owner 2026-10-10 and the FestivalNativeApps verdict, for navigation only) and the menu-button rule of 2026-10-20 where the tabs sit at the top; both change with the lanes that build them.
Rule home: `.agents/patterns/modal-sheet.md` (a page link closes the sheet), `.agents/patterns/page-base.md` (the global chrome); `ipados.md` and `split-view.md` with their own lanes.
Gate: matches_request=yes. The owner chose the rule in session on 2026-11-18 ("all pages, not sheets, not modals, should be able to retain system chrome that's marked as global").
