# iPad full-height form sheet stays centred; Close is its exit in portrait

Decision: three options were judged for a sheet with form width and full height that does not follow a drag down on the iPad in portrait. A, two equal custom detents so the sheet sits at the bottom edge and drags closed. B, stay centred, with Close as the exit in portrait. C, stay centred and add an app drag recogniser. The pick is B. A stock `.formSheet` with nothing else set behaves the same (closes on a drag in landscape, stays in portrait; `build/evidence/native-ingest/nt7fu/report.md`, "stock-control"), so the refusal is the system's and the app keeps the system sheet. A lost: it moves most server-UI sheets off the centre against the owner's "on iPad it is a centred form sheet" and HIG Sheets ("each uses a default size, centered over a dimmed background"), it departs from the web's centred dialog (`ha-sfenton-react-dash@7c4ebf9:src/components/core/ModalSheet.tsx:333,595`), and it rests on an undocumented effect of two equal detents. C lost: it replaces a system gesture with app code against motion section 1, and has no rung-0 note. The shipped app at `4fe28061` already behaves as B. HIG Modality is met by the Close button in the top bar ("commonly use a top-toolbar button or swipe down"); HIG Sheets' "Support swiping vertically to dismiss" is unmet in this one pose, a departure the system makes.
Replaces: the unqualified "Swipe down to dismiss." in the Modal row of `.agents/design/motion.md` section 2, and "swipe down still dismisses" for `.full` sheets in `.agents/patterns/modal-sheet.md`.
Rule home: `.agents/design/motion.md` section 2, Modal row.
Gate: app_wide=no. Nothing drawn, worded, animated or navigated changes.
