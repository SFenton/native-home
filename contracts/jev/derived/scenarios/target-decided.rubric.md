# Labelling rubric: design.target_decided (2026-11-02)

Only for changes that are visible (`design.user_visible` true). Judge from the owner's request and the plan together.

**true** (already settled) when:
- the owner names the result they want: points at another screen, layout, app or the web dashboard that already looks or moves that way, or states the look, copy or motion exactly;
- or an existing pattern, a recorded owner decision, web-dashboard parity or an Apple guideline with one answer fixes what it should look, read or move like;
- even if a pick remains about scope (one screen or all), a rule exception, or how to build it.

**false** (still open) when:
- the owner describes a problem or a wish without naming the result ("this feels cluttered", "make it nicer"), and the options on the table differ in what a person would see;
- or two or more visibly different options remain and nothing on record picks between them.

Do not write label words into the text ("already decided", "settled", "open design question").
